What I changed

  • The layout, colors and type are new. The solutions are yours, in the same order, with the same points.
  • I fixed spelling and typing slips, such as Cookoo to cuckoo, Furnance to furnace, Gowler to Glower, Crusifix to crucifix and Universitat to Universität.
  • I reworded a few garbled sentences without changing the action: scenes 1.3, 1.5, 1.7, 5.9, 5.17, 5.19 and 6.1. In 5.18 and 5.19 the warnings to save your game are now marked as danger steps.
  • I dropped one step. In scene 5.7 the last step (“Tah da. Soon the hunt will really begin.”) repeated the one before it and had no points.
  • I merged three steps in scene 6.26 (furnace controls, the Automatisch button and the handle) into one. It is still 4 points.
  • The scene numbered 3.82 is now 3.8b, so it reads as the part of chapter 3 that comes after 3.8.
  • I added up every running total again from the points on each step. The final total is 679. Two printed totals were off: 6.17 printed 283 where it should be 583, and the “changing screen” step in 6.28 printed 636 where it should be 635.
  • The page now has 100 scenes and 413 steps (210 of them earn points). Your copy has 415 list lines, because of the dropped and merged steps and one split.
  • New: scene place names, chapter subtitles, who you play, tick boxes, a progress bar, and six diagrams (the tape splice, the wolf evidence, the clock trick, the two forest routes, the three acts and the door sequences).

What I could not confirm

  • I did not play the game. I checked the wording and the point arithmetic (the points add up to 679, the maximum listed by Sierra Help). I did not check the solutions in the game itself.
  • The door sequences in scene 6.29 come only from your copy. Other walkthroughs I found give different directions, and I could not settle it. Your copy writes “Half” on several doors, which I show as a half turn, and Door Two says “North” where the others say “Forward”.
  • The place names, the chapter subtitles and who you play in each chapter are my reading of the guide’s text, not taken from the game.
  • The guide says to give Michael “all 3 wolf items” without naming them. In the wolf diagram I used the three wolf things it has you collect.
  • The guide gives the eleven words for the tape splice as one list. I grouped them into three sentences by their punctuation.
  • Scene 3.8 says to “repeat the previous step”. I read that as doing the clock trick again so that you can put the keys back.
  • The guide does not say which release of the game it is for.
