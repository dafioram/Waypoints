Missions

Mission 1: Prologue (Pioneer Stations)

+ Skippable on new game+

+ Sentry Gun

Mission 3: Berkley's Docks

+ Training Room

+ Psychiatry Ward

Long underground section

- Countdown starts once terminal activated

- Bottom level unavailable afterwords

Defend the Otago

- Forced

- Must do in one turn

- No autosave

+ Dropship Barrage Fire

Good to have sentrys and a sniper

- Deactivating Cerberus Starts Countdown Timer

Good to have sentrys and a sniper

- Lights out section

+ 5 person APC

- Long Final Part, Catwalks and Queen

Mission 8: The Montero (Berkley's Docks)

- Lights out section. Best to have full squad with a sniper.
