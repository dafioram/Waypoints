The Death Clock

Once you finish the Cerberus objective in Nuclear Protocol (Mission 6), a day counter starts. If it reaches zero, the planet is bombarded and the campaign is lost. The number of days depends on difficulty.

Days on the clock
DifficultyDays at the startAt most left after Missions 7, 8, 9, 11 and 12
Story3017–20
Medium2512–15
Nightmare207–10
HighNo figure found

Those five missions take 10 to 13 days or more in total. Missions 10 and 13 have no figure of their own, and Missions 9 and 12 are noted as leading straight into them. Base events can cost days too, so the last column is a ceiling, not a promise.

Story and Nightmare: Gamepressure and Steam posters. Medium: Gamepressure and two Steam threads. No figure for High turned up in any source. The days for each mission come from the page author.

Before it starts
Days cost nothing against the clock until you finish the Cerberus objective. Steam players say you can wait as long as you like first.
What costs a day
One deployment per day: after a mission you return to the Otago and advance a day before the next. Some base events cost a day or more. Gamepressure suggests planning for about 3 days per new mission.
Switch it off
When you start a new campaign, choose Custom and set “Disable nuclear countdown”. Difficulty can’t be changed mid-campaign, and a Steam poster says an existing save needs a mod or a restart.
In practice
Steam players report finishing Medium runs with about 14 days left.
