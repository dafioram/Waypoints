Walkthrough

93 steps

As Young Link

Steps 1 to 35, before the Master Sword.

Kokiri Forest and the Deku Tree Steps 1 to 5

Hyrule errands, Death Mountain and Goron City Steps 6 to 16

Dodongo’s Cavern and the errands around it Steps 17 to 28

Zora’s River, Zora’s Domain and Jabu-Jabu Steps 29 to 35

As Old Link

Steps 36 to 93, from the Master Sword on. Steps 45 and 81 are marked YL.

The Master Sword and the hookshot Steps 36 to 37

Forest Temple Steps 38 to 42

Epona, the Temple of Time and the Well Steps 43 to 48

Goron City, the crater and the Ice Cavern Steps 49 to 53

Lake Hylia and the Water Temple Steps 54 to 63

Fire Arrows and the Fire Temple Steps 64 to 77

Errands before the Shadow Temple Steps 78 to 84

Shadow Temple Steps 85 to 90

The desert and Gerudo training Steps 91 to 93

The steps stop at 93, with Gerudo training. Spirit Temple, Ganon’s Castle and Ganondorf have no steps yet; your playthrough times do list them.
