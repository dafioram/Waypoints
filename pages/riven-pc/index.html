Endings

The game can be finishable after solving the Fire Marble puzzle.

  1. Open the Star Fissure before visiting Ghen or Catherine.
  2. Visit Ghen and capture him in the trap book and go back and open the Star Fissure without freeing Catherine.
  3. Visit Ghen and do not capture him, and do not free Catherine, but go directly back and open the Star Fissure. Ghen shoots you.
  4. When Ghen holds the trap book up for you to go first, refuse to link. Return two more times and refuse both times.
  5. After capturing Ghen in the trap book, touch it yourself.
  6. After capturing Ghen in the trap book, return to the Moiety Age and touch it.
  7. Touch the trap book when Keta gives it to you in the Moiety Age.
  8. After Keta gives you the trap book, return to Riven and touch it before visiting Ghen. Ghen shoots you.
  9. Visit Ghen, then go to the Moiety Age and get the trap book. Touch the book before going back to Ghen. Get shot by Ghen. Very similar to Ending 8.
  10. Get trap book, trap Ghen, save Catherine, and open fissure. This is the best ending.
