Series Reference Guide · 2008–2023

Dead Space Games in Release Order

Three main survival-horror games, three spin-offs, and a 2023 remake. This guide covers release order, playtimes, weapons and RIG abilities, New Game+ rules, DLC, platform differences, and the community patches that keep the PC versions running smoothly.

Short answer on platforms: the main campaigns of Dead Space 1, 2, and 3 have the same levels, enemies, and bosses on PC, Xbox 360, and PS3. The differences are extras: one story DLC (Dead Space 2: Severed) never came to PC, the PS3 got a bonus HD copy of Extraction in special editions, and each console had its own cosmetic bonuses. On PC, community patches like MarkerPatch (DS2) and Ishimura Patch (2023 Remake) are strongly recommended.

Story order differs from release order: Extraction is a prequel to the first game, Dead Space (mobile) and Ignition take place shortly before Dead Space 2 (with Mobile ordered before DS2 here to match its prequel timing and simultaneous launch), and the Severed DLC runs alongside Dead Space 2. None of the spin-offs are required to follow the main plot.

Difficulties & unlock requirements

Fresh-start rule for top difficulties: Across the entire series, every game's hardest / permadeath tier (Impossible in DS1 & Remake, Hard in Mobile, Hard Core in DS2, and Classic / Pure Survival / Hardcore in DS3) requires a fresh save from scratch — you cannot carry New Game+ gear into them on your first clear. The only exception is chaining an Impossible NG+ in the 2023 Remake after you have already beaten Impossible fresh.

Beating it unlocks the Burnished Suit, Hand Cannon, and Impossible-to-Impossible NG+ (IGN).

New Game+ comparison

Which games let you raise the difficulty with your NG+ gear? Three of them: Dead Space 2, Dead Space 3, and the 2023 Remake all let you change or bump up standard difficulty on a New Game+ slot (for example, moving from Normal/Medium up to Zealot or Hard with your upgraded arsenal). However, all three still lock their top challenge/permadeath modes (Hard Core, Impossible, and DS3's Classic / Pure Survival / Hardcore) to fresh saves. The 2008 original is the main entry that strictly locks "Round 2" to the exact difficulty you beat it on.

Impossible (permadeath) must be beaten from a fresh save first; beating it unlocks the Burnished Suit & Hand Cannon and lets you chain NG+ off that Impossible save.

Weapons & abilities across the series

Do all Dead Space games have Kinesis and Stasis? Every shooter in the series includes both Stasis (slowing enemies and machinery) and Kinesis (telekinesis for grabbing and throwing objects) — Dead Space Ignition is the only game without them, since it is purely a hacking/puzzle spin-off. Across the main games, those two core tools evolved significantly: Dead Space 2 introduced passive Stasis recharge, lethal Kinesis impalement, and free-flight Zero-G thrusters; Dead Space 3 added combat rolls, cover, and the Scavenger Bot; and the 2023 Remake brought DS2's impalement and Zero-G flight back to the Ishimura alongside a damaging Prototype Stasis upgrade.

Essential PC (Steam) fixes & patches

All four PC releases are playable on modern Windows, but the older ports have frame-rate and input quirks tied to 30 FPS console assumptions, and the 2023 Remake suffers from asset-streaming traversal stutters. Apply these fixes before starting a run:

Dead Space (2008) Steam / PC

  • Dead Space Mouse Fix: Drop in the dinput8.dll raw-mouse fix from PCGamingWiki to eliminate sluggish, frame-rate-tied aim acceleration (Steam Troubleshooter).
  • Fix the 30 FPS VSync & invisible door bug: Turn off in-game VSync (which caps at 30 FPS with heavy lag) and force VSync plus a 60 FPS cap in your NVIDIA/AMD driver panel. Uncapped high FPS breaks physics and causes an invisible wall at the first door after the Plasma Cutter (Steam Discussions).
  • Widescreen Fixer (optional): Lets you widen the narrow default field of view (Steam Troubleshooter).

Dead Space 2 (2011) Steam / PC

  • MarkerPatch (essential): All-in-one modern PC compatibility patch by Wemino for Steam and EA App.
  • What it fixes: Prevents crashes on CPUs with more than 10 cores, replaces the 30 FPS VSync cap with proper monitor refresh-rate VSync, stabilizes Havok physics above 100 FPS, adds true raw mouse input (including Zero-G), increases corpse persistence so loot doesn't despawn, silences dead EA server popups, and adds SDL3 controller + gyro aiming support (Steam Guide).
  • Bonus toggles: Lets you disable the overpowered free DLC store items on a fresh save and unlock the console-exclusive Ignition Conduit Rooms (Steam Guide).

Dead Space 3 (2013) Steam / PC

  • Unlock 30 FPS VSync cap: Disable VSync in-game, ensure Window.VSync = false in system.txt, and force VSync/frame cap through your GPU control panel (Steam Guide).
  • Disable floaty mouse smoothing: Open general.txt in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EA Games\Dead Space 3 and add control.MouseSmoothing = false (Steam Guide).

Dead Space Remake (2023) Steam / PC

  • Ishimura Patch (recommended): Community traversal-stutter fix by lukeman3000 on Nexus Mods targeting the heavy frame-time spikes when crossing between areas on the seamless Ishimura.
  • Measured impact: Independent testing by Digital Foundry showed major frame-time spikes dropping from over 120ms to under 25ms. Note that it is actively developed in beta, so check the mod page for current install notes.

Game-by-game guide

Dead Space 2008 ~11 hrs main

The original. You play engineer Isaac Clarke, alone on the mining ship USG Ishimura, fighting Necromorphs by cutting off their limbs. Slow, tense, and ammo-starved.

Type
Third-person survival horror, single player (12 chapters).
Playtime
~11 hours main story, ~13 hours main + extras, ~20 hours completionist (HowLongToBeat).
Platforms
Xbox 360 and PS3 on October 14, 2008; PC about six days later. The 360 version also runs on newer Xbox consoles through backward compatibility.
Weapons
7 fixed engineering/mining tools: Plasma Cutter, Line Gun, Pulse Rifle, Ripper, Flamethrower, Force Gun, and Contact Beam (IGN).
Abilities
Stasis (manual recharge via wall stations or Stasis Packs), Kinesis (grab and hurl objects/explosives, without DS2's lethal wall-pinning impalement), Zero-G Jump (magnetic boots jumping surface-to-surface rather than free flight), melee punch/stomp, and RIG objective locator.
New Game+
Locked to original difficulty. "Round 2" carries over your gear, upgrades, and credits, and awards 50,000 credits, 10 Power Nodes, backstory logs, and the Level 6 Military Suit schematic (99,000 credits) (IGN). Impossible difficulty unlocks after beating the game, but requires a fresh save (IGN).
DLC
No story DLC. Xbox 360 got a batch of small, cheap packs (weapon skins, suits, minor stat boosts) shortly after launch. Each console also had its own exclusive bonus suit (black on PS3, grey on 360).
Online
None.
Systems & PC
Same game, levels, and bosses everywhere. On PC, install the Dead Space Mouse Fix and cap FPS at 60 via your GPU driver (with in-game VSync off) to avoid the 30 FPS lock and the invisible-door physics bug (Steam Troubleshooter).

Dead Space: Extraction 2009 ~6 hrs main

A light-gun style rail shooter prequel set during the Aegis VII outbreak, before the first game. You don't control movement; you aim and shoot while the game walks you through the horror.

Type
On-rails shooter with story cutscenes, local drop-in co-op, branching paths, and a strong horror tone.
Playtime
~6 hours main story, ~7 hours main + extras, ~13 hours completionist (HowLongToBeat).
Platforms
Wii in 2009. An HD PS3 version with PlayStation Move support came with Dead Space 2's pre-order bonus and special editions, and also as a standalone release.
Weapons
10 weapons: the returning 7 from Dead Space (Plasma Cutter, Line Gun, Pulse Rifle, Ripper, Flamethrower, Force Gun, Contact Beam) plus the unlimited-ammo 935 Bonder Rivet Gun, the P-Sec Pistol (Divet), and the exclusive Arc Welder electric cone gun (Dead Space Wiki).
Abilities
Stasis (wrist-mounted unit), Kinesis (snatching loot and throwing hazards), the exclusive Glow Worm (shake the controller for temporary light), plus motion-tilted alt-fire and melee swings (Dead Space Wiki).
New Game+
No traditional NG+. Completing the campaign unlocks Chapter Select (retaining weapon upgrades), Impossible difficulty, and wave-based Challenge Mode.
Systems
Same story on both; the PS3 version is a higher-resolution re-release playable with either PlayStation Move or a standard DualShock 3. Its two leads, Gabe Weller and Lexine Murdoch, return in the Severed DLC.

Dead Space Ignition 2010 ~1.5 hrs main

A downloadable puzzle and hacking game told through motion-comic style scenes. It sets up the start of Dead Space 2 and was released a few months before it.

Type
Interactive comic with hacking and puzzle mini-games. Not a shooter.
Playtime
~1.5 hours for one run, ~2.5 hours main + extras, ~4.5 hours for all 4 endings and completionist (HowLongToBeat).
Platforms
PS3 (October 12, 2010) and Xbox 360 (October 13, 2010), digital only. No longer available to buy.
Weapons & Abilities
No weapons, Stasis, or Kinesis. Gameplay consists strictly of three arcade hacking minigames: Hardware Crack (rotating reflectors to route colored lasers), System Override (reverse tower-defense deploying four virus types), and Trace Route (side-scrolling electrical signal race with boost/firewall/reverse power-ups) (Game Informer).
New Game+
None — replays simply branch down different story forks to see all four endings and unlock the Hacker Suit and Conduit Rooms in Dead Space 2.
Verdict
Skippable. Its main role was to set up the opening of Dead Space 2.

Dead Space (mobile / Sabotage) 2011 ~2–3 hrs

Also known as Dead Space: Sabotage, Dead Space iOS, or Dead Space Mobile. It shares its name with the 2008 game, but it is not a remake or port — it's a new story built for phones, set shortly before Dead Space 2 and released on the same day. It reuses many art assets from the original, and its tram stations look nearly identical to the Ishimura's.

Developer
IronMonkey Studios, EA's Australian mobile studio, with help from Visceral Games, published by Electronic Arts. Production took about a year. Antony Johnston wrote the story and Jason Graves, the series composer, did the music.
Type
Chapter-based third-person survival-horror shooter, single player, with 12 chapters. The team considered an on-rails shooter, but chose a real third-person game with touch controls built around the series' minimal on-screen interface. Later updates added Survival and Endless modes on higher-end devices.
Playtime
~2–3 hours for a full playthrough.
Setting
Titan Station in 2511, a precursor to Ignition, Dead Space 2, and Severed. You play Karrie Norton (codename Vandal), not Isaac. It's a side story, so you don't need it for the main plot.
Weapons
6 weapons total (5 on Android): the exclusive close-range SH-B1 Plasma Saw, Plasma Cutter, Line Gun, Ripper, the exclusive Contact Beam–style Core Extractor, and the Heavy Pulse Rifle (200,000 credits in the Store; iOS/iPad only, never released on Android) (Dead Space Wiki).
Abilities
Stasis, Kinesis, Zero-G Jump, RIG Locator, stomp/melee, and auto-regenerating health (unique in the series — there are no Med Packs) (Dead Space Wiki).
New Game+
No difficulty-bump NG+. Purchased upgrade modules and unlocked Survival/Endless modes persist across runs.
Gameplay
The usual Store and Bench for ammo, suits (RIGs), and weapon upgrades, with a few twists: you can't sell weapons back, health regenerates automatically, and each of the four core ranged weapons has a hard ammo cap that bench upgrades raise. Most weapons are found in lockers; the Heavy Pulse Rifle is the only one you buy. Features enhanced enemies (including enhanced Pregnant and Exploder variants) plus a single reactor-core boss.
Platforms
iPhone and iPod touch on January 25, 2011 ($6.99), plus an iPad edition, Dead Space HD ($9.99). Android, Kindle Fire, and BlackBerry PlayBook versions followed in August 2011. Xperia Play controller support was added and later removed.
In-app purchases
Credit packs ($0.99–$6.99), power node packs ($0.99–$4.99), and three $1.99 modules (Credit Booster, Armor Integrity, Weapon Output). Registering with an EA account granted a free Power Node in Dead Space 2.
Reception
Mostly positive. Reviewers praised the visuals, sound, and console-style feel on a phone, with minor complaints about camera/interface quirks and repetition. Nominated for Mobile Game of the Year at the D.I.C.E. Awards.
Availability
Delisted. Pulled from the iOS App Store in September 2015; the Android release was renamed zzSunset Dead Space and won't launch on newer devices because it tries to fetch a mandatory update from offline EA servers (and Android Lollipop had major audio issues).
Fun facts
The first letters of the 12 chapters spell a hidden message (H.E. W.I.L.L. B.E.T.R.A.Y.), continuing series tradition. It also made movie cameos in The Darkest Hour and Krampus.

Dead Space 2 2011 ~9 hrs main

Isaac wakes up three years later on the Sprawl, a space city on Titan. Faster, more cinematic, and more action-driven than the first game, and often named the series' best.

Type
Third-person survival horror with more action. 15 chapters.
Playtime
~9 hours main story, ~16.5 hours completionist (HowLongToBeat). Severed DLC adds ~1.5–2 hours.
Platforms
Xbox 360, PS3, and PC, January 2011. The Xbox 360 version also runs on newer Xbox consoles through backward compatibility.
Weapons
10 weapons + 1 unlockable: the original 7 (Plasma Cutter, Line Gun, Pulse Rifle, Ripper, Flamethrower, Force Gun, Contact Beam) plus four newcomers — Javelin Gun, Detonator (trip mines), Seeker Rifle, and 711-MarkCL Rivet Gun (DLC/bonus) — and the Hand Cannon foam finger for beating Hard Core (Dead Space Wiki).
Abilities
Auto-recharging Stasis (slowly refills passively over time), Upgraded Kinesis with Impalement (skewering and pinning Necromorphs with sharp blades/poles), Free-Flight Zero-G Thrusters (replacing DS1's point-to-point jumps), panel Hacking minigames, and faster stomp/melee (Kinesis / Stasis Wiki).
New Game+
Can increase difficulty (Casual, Normal, Survivalist, Zealot). Carries over all weapons, suits, upgrades, credits, Power Nodes, inventory, and Safe items, and unlocks Elite Suit schematics plus the free Riot Security Suit (Dead Space Wiki). Beating Zealot unlocks the Arctic Security Suit (Dead Space Wiki). Hard Core (only 3 saves, no checkpoints) cannot use an NG+ save and forces a fresh start, rewarding the Soldier Suit and Hand Cannon (Dead Space Wiki). Severed has no NG+.
DLC
Severed (March 2011, ~$7): two short chapters following Gabe Weller and Lexine Murdoch from Extraction. Xbox 360 and PS3 only; it never came to PC.
Supernova, Hazard, and Martial Law packs (~$5 each): weapon and suit bundles (included free on PC).
Outbreak Map Pack: two multiplayer maps (The Academy, The Concourse).
Collector's Edition pack: Zealot suit and Force Gun / Contact Beam bonuses.
Online
Competitive multiplayer mode (humans vs. Necromorphs). EA shut down the servers on December 8, 2023 for PC, PS3, and Xbox 360, so only the campaign is playable now.
Systems & PC
Same campaign on all three; consoles have Severed and PS3 special editions bundled HD Extraction. On PC (Steam/EA App), install MarkerPatch to fix the >10-core CPU launch crash, 30 FPS VSync cap, Havok high-FPS physics bugs, raw mouse input in Zero-G, dead server popups, and optional Conduit Room unlocks (Steam Guide).

Dead Space 3 2013 ~12.5 hrs main

Isaac, with a new partner, heads to the icy planet Tau Volantis to end the Marker threat. A big shift in style: more shooting, human enemies, cover, outdoor areas, and a deep weapon-crafting bench.

Type
Action horror. Playable solo, or in two-player drop-in co-op as Isaac and John Carver. Co-op has exclusive side missions and extra story scenes you don't get alone.
Playtime
~12.5 hours main story, ~16.5 hours main + extras, ~25 hours completionist (HowLongToBeat). Awakened DLC adds ~1.5–2 hours (HowLongToBeat).
Platforms
Xbox 360, PS3, and PC, February 5, 2013.
Weapons
Modular Weapon Crafting Bench: Combine Compact or Heavy Frames with Upper & Lower Tools, Tips, Attachments (such as Stasis Coating or Acid Bath), and Upgrade Circuits to recreate classic guns (Plasma Cutter, Pulse Rifle, Ripper, Line Gun, Force Gun, Contact Beam, Flamethrower, Javelin, Rivet Gun) or invent hybrids (Shotgun, SMG, Telemetry Spike, Tesla Core, Bolas, Rocket/Grenade Launchers) using universal ammo (IGN). You carry only 2 weapons at a time, plus the unlockable Devil Horns foam finger.
Abilities
Auto-recharging Stasis (plus Stasis Coating weapon mods and late-game Stasis/Kinesis Amplification Fields), Kinesis (impalement + catching/returning enemy grenades), Free-Flight Zero-G Thrusters, Crouch / Cover & Combat Dodge Roll, Scavenger Bot (deployable radar-guided drone that harvests crafting resources and returns them to Benches), and co-op item sharing/revives (Game Informer, Dead Space Wiki).
New Game+
Can change/increase difficulty on Standard NG+ (Casual, Normal, Hard, Impossible), keeping all weapons, parts, circuits, resources, suits, RIG upgrades, and logs while spawning new high-tier Upgrade Circuits. Also unlocks three fresh-start post-game modes: Classic (Hard, solo, no crafting, classic blueprints only, unlocks Devil Horns), Pure Survival (Hard, zero health/ammo drops — craft everything), and Hardcore (infinite saves, permadeath on a single death, unlocks Retro Mode) (Dead Space Wiki).
DLC
Awakened (March 12, 2013, ~$10): the only story DLC. A shorter, darker, more horror-focused epilogue, playable solo or in co-op.
Plus many suit/weapon packs and microtransactions for crafting resources, which drew heavy criticism at launch.
Online
Co-op requires an online connection. Check EA's online services shutdown page for current status, as no shutdown has been announced.
Systems & PC
Same campaign everywhere; Awakened is on all three platforms. Xbox 360 supports Kinect voice/gesture commands; PS3 has no Move support. On PC, disable in-game VSync (Window.VSync = false in system.txt) to bypass the 30 FPS cap and add control.MouseSmoothing = false to general.txt (Steam Guide).

Dead Space (remake) 2023 ~12 hrs main

A ground-up rebuild of the 2008 game by Motive Studio, not a port. It follows the same story and chapter structure, but plays like a modernized fusion of Dead Space 1 and 2.

Type
Third-person survival horror, single player only (12 chapters).
Playtime
~12 hours main story, ~30.5 hours completionist (HowLongToBeat).
Platforms
PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, EA app), January 27, 2023.
Weapons
The classic 7 — Plasma Cutter, Pulse Rifle, Line Gun, Ripper, Flamethrower, Force Gun, and Contact Beam — all with reworked alt-fires (such as Pulse Rifle proximity mines, Force Gun gravity wells, and Line Gun laser traps) and discoverable special Bench upgrades (IGN), plus the unlockable Hand Cannon (IGN).
Abilities
Stasis (manual recharge like 2008, plus an optional side-mission Prototype Stasis Module that shocks and strips Necromorph flesh over time), Kinesis with Impalement (brought back from DS2), Free-Flight Zero-G Thrusters (replacing 2008's magnetic jumps), Circuit Breakers (rerouting power between lights, oxygen, and doors), melee/stomp, and RIG Locator.
New Game+
Can change/increase difficulty among Story, Easy, Medium, and Hard — fixing the 2008 original's lock. Awards 50,000 credits, 10 Power Nodes, and the Level 6 Advanced Soldier RIG (99,000 credits), while adding tougher Phantom Necromorphs (Dead Space Wiki) and 12 Marker Fragments for a secret alternate ending (IGN). Impossible (Hard + 1 save slot + permadeath) must be started fresh; beating it unlocks the Burnished Suit and Hand Cannon and allows chaining NG+ off that Impossible save (Steam Guide).
What's new
The entire Ishimura is one seamless, loading-free ship you can backtrack across. An "Intensity Director" dynamically alters scares and enemy spawns so cleared rooms never feel completely safe. Isaac is fully voiced, there are side missions and expanded lore for Nicole, and a "peeling" damage system strips flesh off Necromorphs to expose bone.
DLC / online
No story DLC and no multiplayer; Digital Deluxe adds cosmetic suit textures only.
Systems & PC
Identical content on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. On PC, install the community Ishimura Patch to dramatically reduce traversal stutters (Digital Foundry).

None of the platform versions of the three original games have extra levels, tougher bosses, or exclusive endings. Difficulty modes (including the harsh Hard Core and Impossible settings) are the same on each. "More polished" mostly means resolution, frame rate, and loading times, so a patched PC version or a current Xbox (via backward compatibility) is the smoothest way to play the originals.

Other media and where the series stands

Beyond games there is a browser game (No Known Survivors, 2008), two animated films (Downfall and Aftermath), comics, a graphic novel, and novels. They add lore but aren't needed.

As of late 2026 there's no new Dead Space game announced. Reports since 2024 say EA put the franchise on hold after the remake missed its sales targets. A Dead Space 2 remake has been rumored, but EA hasn't confirmed one, so treat any dates you see as speculation.

What to play

  • New to the series: play the 2023 Remake (with the Ishimura Patch on PC), then Dead Space 2 (with MarkerPatch on PC), then Dead Space 3.
  • Want the full Dead Space 2 package: an Xbox 360 or PS3 copy (or Xbox backward compatibility) is the only way to play Severed.
  • Want the best horror from the sequels: Dead Space 3 with the Awakened DLC, played solo (or try Classic Mode on NG+).
  • Completionist: add Extraction (rail shooter) for Aegis VII lore. Ignition and the mobile game are delisted and hard to play today.
