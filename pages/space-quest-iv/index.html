Space Quest IV

A complete walkthrough for Space Quest IV, including all point-scoring actions and a final score of 315 points.

Overview & Controls

  • F2 — Sound toggle
  • F5 — Save
  • F7 — Load
  • F9 — Restart
  • CTRL+Q — Quit

Use the mouse to control Wilco. The menu at the top handles inventory and settings. The right mouse button cycles through commands, and the left mouse button activates the selected command.

Point totals are shown inline as (+points/total). Negative points are shown where applicable.

Space Quest XII

  • After the introduction, go right and take the rope. (+5/5)
  • Place the rope where the bunny enters the screen and catch the bunny. (+10/20)
  • Go right twice and open the skimmer.
  • Open the glove compartment and take the laptop computer. (+5/25)
  • Look at the rabbit and remove the battery from its back. (+3/28)
  • Look at the laptop and insert the battery. (+3/31)
  • Go up and use the tank.
  • Take the unstable ordnance. (+25/56)
  • Put the unstable ordnance back. (-20/36)
  • Go right twice and open the grate to enter the sewers. (+5/41)
  • Take the jar from the table. (+5/46)
  • Press the button under the blotter and watch the hologram projection. (+10/56)
  • Open the door on the left and continue through the sewers until you reach the ladder.
  • Wait close to the ladder until the slime catches up.
  • Collect some goo with the jar and quickly climb the ladder. (+5/61)
  • Leave the sewers when the Sequel Police arrive. (+3/64)
  • Go east and enter the ship underneath the landing gear. (+5/69)
  • After landing, go left and enter the pod before the guards spot you. (+10/79)
  • Enter the copyright-protection code if required. Write down the displayed code.
  • Enter the code to lift off and warp to Chapter 10.

Space Quest X

  • Press the red button to leave the ship and go left.
  • Walk into the shadow and let the bird fly past.
  • Go back right and down the stairs to the nest.
  • Wait for the Sequel Police officer to be dropped, then search him for a chewing-gum wrapper. (+5/84)
  • Exit the nest through the hole at the bottom right.
  • Enter the pod and drive to the cave.
  • Press the red button to zap the tentacles. (+5/89)
  • Quickly pick up the oxygen tank. (+5/94)
  • When the sea monster grabs you, throw the oxygen tank into its mouth. (+5/99)
  • Continue to the Galaxy Galleria.
  • Go right and follow the moving walkway. Pick up the credit card at the start of the area. (+2/101)
  • Enter the Software store.
  • Look in the bargain bin and find the Space Quest IV hint book.
  • Keep the book and pay the storekeeper. (+5/106)
  • Go left to the Big and Tall store.
  • Ask the clerk for clothes and pay him. (+5/111)
  • Go right into Monolith Burger.
  • Talk to the manager until he offers you a job.
  • Work until the manager fires you. (+3/114)
  • Make sure your total cash, including your previous money, reaches $60.
  • Follow the cigar butt right and pick it up. (+5/119)
  • Go left and enter Sacks.
  • Talk to the robot clerk and change into women's clothing.
  • Pay the $60. (+5/124)
  • Go right two screens and use the credit card on the ATM.
  • Select Clean House to receive the $2001. (+10/134)
  • Return to Sacks and enter changing room 3. (+3/137)
  • Go east and enter the Arcade.
  • Go to the top right to trigger the Sequel Police.
  • Avoid the police officer who remains in the Arcade and go outside.
  • Turn right until you can enter Skate-O-Rama.

Skate-O-Rama

Floppy Disk Version:

  • Walk into the zero-G zone and immediately move straight upward.
  • Keep moving while the Sequel Policeman shoots at you.
  • Move upward into the dome.
  • When the police arrive, move back down to the bottom and return to the stairs.
  • Return to the moving walkway and go back to the Arcade.

CD-ROM Version:

  • Walk into the zero-G zone and immediately move straight down.
  • Keep moving while the Sequel Policeman shoots.
  • At the bottom of the screen, rapidly alternate between the WAIT and WALK icons.
  • Move to the right edge, then down.
  • Move straight up, dodging the top-right corner, and enter the dome.
  • When the police enter the dome, move back down to the skating rink and onto the stairs.
  • If they do not enter the dome, move slightly upward and then back down.
  • Return to the walkway and the Arcade.
  • Enter the time pod the police arrived in and write down its displayed code. (+15/152)
  • Combine page 4 of the SQ4 hint book with the chewing-gum wrapper and enter the resulting code. (+5/157)

Space Quest I

  • Press the red button to exit the pod and go south.
  • Enter the bar and get thrown out by the bikers.
  • Push the bikes over to get their attention. (+5/162)
  • Return to the bar.
  • Dodge a biker just before he hits you. (+5/167)
  • Take the matchbook inside the bar. (+5/172)
  • Return to the ship.
  • Enter the original code to return to Space Quest XII.

Space Quest XII

  • Open the door with the red button and go right past the ship you arrived in.
  • Use the jar of slime on the lock. (+10/182)
  • Light the cigar with the matches and smoke it. (+10/192)
  • Use the keypad on the wall and enter the laser codes:
  1. Laser 1: 156
  2. Laser 2: 024
  3. Laser 3: 108
  • Walk through the tunnel and inspect the nearby terminal.
  • Write down the plug information.
  • Return to the time pod and enter the code for Space Quest X.

Space Quest X

  • Open the door with the red button and enter Radio Shock.
  • Select Catalog → Electronic Gadgets.
  • Continue until the PocketPal appears.
  • Order the PocketPal plug matching the terminal information. (+15/222)
  • If you bought the wrong plug, exchange it.
  • Return to the pod in the Arcade.
  • Use the code to return to Space Quest XII.

Space Quest XII

  • Open the pod door with the red button and return to the terminal.
  • Use the PocketPal plug with the PocketPal. (+3/225)
  • Use the PocketPal on the terminal. (+10/235)
  • Turn off the PocketPal to leave the screen.
  • Make your way west to the large building while avoiding the flying droids. (+10/245)
  • Open the building door using the SQ4 hint-book code: 6965847669. (+10/255)
  • Enter the control room.
  • Dump the droid into the toilet. (+5/260)
  • Dump the brain into the toilet. (+15/275)
  • A countdown begins.
  • Close the window and follow this route to Vohaul:

Right → Right → Right → Up → Left → Down the steps → Left into elevator → North → Right into tunnel (+5/280)

  • Fight Vohaul by clicking on his back whenever he gets close. Do this four times.
  • Climb down the ladder and retrieve the disk. (+5/285)
  • Insert the disk into the drive. (+5/290)
  • Select Disk Upload for your son's information.
  • Select Beam Upload for Vohaul's information.
  • Select BEAM DOWNLOAD to restore your son's information to his body. (+25/315)
  • Watch the ending.
