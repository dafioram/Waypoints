How to read this

The game calls a day a chapter. Each day is a set of scenes, one per place, with the steps in order.

Do this+388

+3 is what the step earns. 88 is your total after it. Steps with nothing there earn no points.

Tick me

Tick a step when you have done it. The page keeps your ticks and your score in this browser.

Next

A bar down the left edge marks the first step you have not ticked, once you have ticked something. The Resume button jumps there.

The bar above the days has one block per day, all the same width. A block fills as you tick that day’s points.

Danger

Save your game first. These steps, or the note after them, can get Gabriel killed or end the game.

Heads up

A note about a freeze, a trap or lost points. You do not tick it.

Take the flashlight

Bold words in a step are things you pick up or use.

Boxed picture

A boxed picture explains the step or scene near it. The words are always in the step as well.

Before you start

  • Keep a fresh save at the start of every day, whether or not you want all 342 points. Spare saves get you out of the traps below.
  • Patch the game first. The guide says most people now play through DOSBox and that the patches cure glitches such as the freeze at the start of Day 5. It gave a link to a patch collection. I left it out because I could not check where it leads.
  • Some events end a day, and others can wait many days. Steps are listed at the earliest time you can do them, even when the use comes much later. The flashlight on Day 1 is not used until Day 7.

Controls

Left click uses the icon you have selected. Right click cycles through the icons, but picking one from the menu at the top of the screen is faster unless you are very good at cycling.

The lever icon in that menu opens the main menu. It has R (restore a save) and S (save), and lets you change walking speed, sound and music levels, and animated detail. On CD-ROM versions you can also switch voices. The DOS version without the CD has no voices by default.

Common problems

How come I cannot talk about St. John’s Eve?
Go to Dixieland Drug Store in the French Quarter and read the lagniappe notice on the left-hand counter. That adds it as a topic for Dr. John, Magentia and others.
I click Exit but Gabriel does not leave.
The cursor has to be right at the edge of the screen. Sometimes the Exit prompt appears and still needs the cursor placed just so. Walking Gabriel along the screen edge also works.
On Day 9, how do I get past the things in the mound?
Turn the Speed slider all the way up. The guide says it makes a huge difference.

What I changed

  • I rewrote every step in my own words. The solutions are the guide’s, in the same order, with the same points.
  • I added up every running total again from the points on each step. All 190 of them match the totals printed in the guide, and the last one is 342.
  • The page says Sins of the Fathers, which is the game’s title. The guide’s heading says “Father”.
  • I fixed spellings: Napolean to Napoleon and Caxaunoux to Cazaunoux. I spell veve one way (the guide has both veve and vévé) and wrote out “Caza” as Cazaunoux.
  • The guide has 53 scene headings. The page has 58 scenes, because Day 9 now has three and Day 10 has five, where the guide gave each one or two headings. Every scene has a number.
  • Day 3 ended with an empty heading and a note. It is now a scene with one step, watching the scene with Malia, and the note under it.
  • On Day 9 the two steps about putting stone 1 in its slot are now one step.
  • In scene 9.2 the guide says to go “north” to room 3. I say it is the next room clockwise from room 2, which is what its map shows.
  • The guide’s intro links to a shortened address for patches. I dropped the link, because I could not check where it goes.
  • New: the attic clock solution, which the guide leaves out (from GameBoomers, matched by Sierra Help). Also new: place names for scenes, day subtitles, how each day ends, tick boxes, a progress bar, and pictures for the mime, the clock, the phone numbers, the tomb message, the snake mound and the lair doors.
  • The page has 246 steps, and 190 of them earn points.

What I could not confirm

  • I did not play the game. I checked the wording and the point arithmetic against the guide’s own printed totals. I did not check the solutions in the game itself, and I only ran the page in Chromium.
  • The 342-point maximum comes from a player thread on GOG, not from the publisher. The guide’s own totals reach it exactly.
  • The place names for scenes, the day subtitles and the names of the Day 9 and Day 10 scenes are my reading of the guide’s text, not taken from the game. “Wheel within a wheel” is the guide’s own heading.
  • The guide does not say how to solve the attic clock. The steps I give are from the GameBoomers walkthrough, and Sierra Help gives the same three moves. Sierra Help does not mention a drawer. I did not try it.
  • The guide says to drum “Summon Brother Eagle” using “both books” without naming them. The note under that step is how GameBoomers describes it.
  • The snake mound map in the guide lists a stone in room 7, and the note above it says stones that need no moving are not listed. The steps say to leave stone 7 alone. I followed the steps. The map also shows one ring of twelve rooms, while the text speaks of a twin ring with twelve rooms in each.
  • The guide calls the item “snake staff” when you pick it up and “snake rod” when you use it. I took them to be the same item and used “snake rod”.
  • The guide never says where the Day 9 knife comes from. GameBoomers has Gabriel take a large knife from the staircase wall on Day 7, and I guess it is the dagger from the guide’s Day 7 steps.
  • The guide says to use the snake rod on the confessional bench “(left behind)” and to “give Mosely the boar outfit”. I could not tell what “left behind” refers to, and I took “outfit” to mean the mask and robe together.
  • On Day 3 the guide says to sell “the painting” to Bruno, but no earlier step gets a painting. GameBoomers says a neighboring shop owner comes in to buy it and does not name him. I did not check either.
  • The guide’s author says the manual gives the tomb message answer outright. I could not check that.
  • The guide names no author and no release, and I could not find where it was first published. I do not know which patch it means.
