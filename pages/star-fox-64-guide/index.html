Star Fox 64 Routes, challenges and endings

16 stages, 25 routes and two endings. Nine of the levels are forks. Clear the challenge in one and a harder branch opens. Miss it and you take the easy branch. Pick a level on the chart to see every route that passes through it.

Nintendo 64, released in Japan on 27 April 1997 and in North America on 23 June 1997.

The two endings

Bolse leads to Venom 1 and Area 6 leads to Venom 2. Nothing else decides the ending.

Venom 1

Reached from Bolse. 14 of the 25 routes end here.

Fox flies the temple alone and destroys the android Andross, then escapes. The credits show Andross laughing, still alive. It is the ending for the easy lane.

Final fights
Golemech, then a robot Andross
Difficulty
4 of 5
Medal
200 hits

Venom 2

Reached from Area 6. 11 of the 25 routes end here.

After Andross’s brain falls, you follow James McCloud, Fox’s father, out of the collapsing lair. After the credits, THE END appears. The wiki calls this the canonical ending.

Final fights
Star Wolf’s Wolfen II ships, then Andross in his true form
Difficulty
5 of 5
Medal
200 hits

Quick answers

How do I get the real ending?
Get to Area 6. It opens from Macbeth when you derail the train, or from Sector Z when you stop all six missiles. Area 6 leads only to Venom 2, which the wiki calls the canonical ending.
What is the least I have to do?
Venom 1 needs no challenge at all: Corneria, Meteo, Fichina, Sector X, Titania and Bolse with every optional goal left alone. For Venom 2 the fewest is 2 challenges, for example beat Star Wolf at Fichina and derail the train at Macbeth, or take the warp at Sector X and shoot down the missiles at Sector Z, or beat Spyborg quickly at Sector X and derail the train at Macbeth.
Which levels do the most routes pass through?
Sector X is on 15 of the 25 routes, more than any level after the start. Meteo, Katina, Macbeth and Bolse are each on 14. Titania is on only 3, the fewest, then Aquas and Zoness with 4 each.
Do I have to take the harder branch once it opens?
No. The wiki gives 25 routes in all, and that count only works if clearing a challenge adds an option rather than forcing it. Its pages for Zoness, Macbeth, Katina and Sector Y spell out the choice.
What do I need for a medal?
A hit total for the level, from 50 at Fichina to 300 at Area 6, and Falco, Peppy and Slippy all alive when you finish. Medals from every level unlock Expert Mode, which has more enemies that fire more often and breaks your wings on any crash.

Mission 1

Corneria

Enter Star Fox

Setting

Vehicle

Medal
150 hits
Difficulty
1 of 5
Routes
25 of 25

A run over the sea and through the capital city, then a boss fight. Every route starts here, and it holds the first fork.

Boss

Granga. The default boss, in an all-range fight. Shoot a leg so it falls over, then hit the power pack on its back.

Attack Carrier. The boss on the hard path. Destroy its three launchers, then the main ship.

Pickups

  • Gold rings under the arches over the water. Three raise your shield limit.
  • Silver rings from downed fighters.
  • A laser upgrade in the city, from the cluster of enemies you can lock onto with a charged shot.
  • A smart bomb behind one of the arches.
  • An extra Arwing. StrategyWiki lists Corneria, Sector Y and Venom as the levels that have one.

Where it leads

Comes from: The start of the game.

Your challenge

  • Save Falco from the three fighters on his tail, then fly through all seven arches over the water. Follow Falco through the waterfall.
  • Meteo, with Granga as the boss.
  • After the challenge: Sector Y, with the Attack Carrier as the boss.

What changes with your route

  • Falco has to be with you, because he is the one who finds the Attack Carrier.

Mission 2

Meteo

Into the Asteroid Field

Setting

ArwingCorridor
Medal
200 hits
Difficulty
1 of 5

14 of 25

A corridor through a dense asteroid field. Peppy calls out when to brake and boost, and ROB 64 sends supplies partway.

Boss

Meteo Crusher. A big shield on its front reflects your shots, so go for the yellow weak points as they open. It fakes a surrender, so keep firing at the rest of it afterwards.

The warp route. There is no boss. The warp skips the fight.

Pickups

  • A supply container from ROB 64. Press C-right when ROB calls.
  • Laser upgrades in rings of asteroids.
  • Gold rings from the snake enemies and from asteroids that split.
  • Bombs, for flying through the “64” formation (Magna’s guide).
  • A shield star.

Where it leads

Comes from: Corneria, by default.

Your challenge

  • Fly through the blue warp rings near the end, then through the warp zone. The wiki counts seven rings, and one older guide says six.
  • Fichina, after you beat Meteo Crusher.
  • After the challenge: Katina, through the warp. The warp zone also lets you pick Fichina.

What changes with your route

  • The warp area is full of power-ups. Magna’s medal guide calls the warp the easier way to the 200-hit medal.

Sector Y

Fierce Melee

Medal

11 of 25

A battle among burnt-out warships and giant humanoid robots, which General Pepper asks Star Fox to win. It is the first hard level, and it starts the hard lane.

Boss

Two Shogun robots. They share one shield bar. Shoot each until it explodes. A direct bomb hit on any Shogun takes off half the bar.

The piloted Shogun. A larger, faster robot that flies around the arena and docks on its ship. Wait for it to land, then shoot it.

Pickups

  • A gold ring on top of a battleship’s control tower, and another in the last arch.
  • A shield star near the top left.
  • Laser upgrades from the two blue robots.
  • An extra Arwing (see Corneria).

Where it leads

Comes from: Corneria, after the Attack Carrier.

Your challenge

  • Finish with 100 hits or more. Near the end Falco complains if you have under 90, and Peppy praises you if you have more.
  • Taking the upper path at both forks gives you more enemies to shoot.
  • Katina, if you finish with 99 hits or fewer.
  • After the challenge: Aquas, with 100 hits or more. You can still pick Katina.

What changes with your route

  • The route needs 100 hits. The medal needs 150.

An all-range dogfight against Star Wolf, the rival team, while a bomb on the base counts down.

The English Nintendo 64 release calls this planet Fortuna. Later games and the 3DS version call it Fichina.

Boss

Star Wolf. Four Wolfen, one for each pilot. Fly straight to draw one onto your tail, then somersault behind it and fire. Each is worth 10 hits. When all four are down, Fox defuses the bomb.

Pickups

  • Silver rings from the transmitters, if you get in trouble. The guides I read list no other pickups here.

Where it leads

Comes from: Meteo, if you skip the warp.

Your challenge

  • Shoot down all four Wolfen before the bomb goes off.
  • Sector X, if the bomb goes off.
  • After the challenge: Solar, if you beat Star Wolf.

What changes with your route

  • If Star Wolf beats your wingmen in the dogfight, you go straight to Sector X.
  • If you do not beat Star Wolf here, they join the fight at Bolse.

Mission 3

Katina

Reunion

Setting
Frontline base
Vehicle
ArwingAll-range
Medal
150 hits
Difficulty
2 of 5
Routes
14 of 25

Bill Grey, Fox’s academy classmate, calls for help at one of Corneria’s front-line bases. Andross’s fighters are swarming it and a huge mothership hangs overhead.

Boss

Saucerer. Shoot enemies until the mothership appears, then shoot its hatches as they open. The core then appears as an upside-down cone. Destroy it within about a minute, or the mothership destroys the base.

Medal tip. Take out the hatches but leave the core until the last possible second, so you can keep shooting the swarm.

Pickups

  • The guides I read list no pickups here. Points come from the swarm and from the hatches, which release about two dozen enemies between them.

Where it leads

Comes from: Meteo through the warp, or Sector Y with fewer than 100 hits.

Your challenge

  • Destroy the core before the timer runs out.
  • Sector X, if the base is destroyed.
  • After the challenge: Solar, if you win. You can still pick Sector X.

What changes with your route

  • Bill repays you at Solar or Sector X, whichever you pick.
  • Cornerian fighters have pink dots on their backs. Shooting them carries no medal penalty, but each one you down leaves one fewer ship following you at the end. Destroy the core without shooting any and Bill’s farewell changes.

The only level flown in the Blue Marine submarine. Lasers can’t be charged, but torpedoes are unlimited. Clams, squids, anglers and jellyfish guard an underwater bio-weapon.

Boss

Bacoon. A giant clam. Hit one of its membrane supports until it glows white, then fire a torpedo at it. Do the same to the second one, then torpedo the eye. The four parasites on top are worth 4 hits each.

Pickups

  • Silver rings from clams and rocks.
  • Gold rings from pillars and from an angler.
  • A shield star from a squid.
  • A laser upgrade from the last arch.

Where it leads

Comes from: Sector Y, with 100 hits or more.

  • Zoness. It is the only exit.

What changes with your route

  • Zoness can only be reached through Aquas. To get there you have to clear Sector Y with 100 hits first.

Mystery of the Space Base

A half-built space station with big swarms, spiky and insect-like enemies, and laser cannons. The track splits at a fork, and the left side hides a warp.

Boss

Spyborg. Shoot its head when its eyes light up. Dodge the slams, which knock out your wings. When it fakes its death, finish the head before Slippy is knocked out. It is not fought on the warp route.

Pickups

  • A laser upgrade from the last of three drones near the fork.
  • A gold ring inside the energy rings of a moth enemy.
  • A gold ring in a crack in the wall on the way to Sector Z.

Where it leads

Comes from: Fichina or Katina.

Your challenge

  • The warp: take the left fork, then shoot and fly through all four gates. Falco helps you find it.
  • The quick kill: beat Spyborg quickly, before it knocks Slippy into space.
  • Titania, if Spyborg takes too long and Slippy is lost.
  • After the challenge: Macbeth, after a quick kill. You can still pick Titania.
  • After the challenge: Sector Z, through the warp.

What changes with your route

  • Bill Grey appears here only if you came through Katina. One GameFAQs walkthrough says he helps open the four gates.

A flight over lava waves, hills and rock. Fly too low and the heat damages your Arwing.

Boss

Vulcain. A lava hill rises to show it. Destroy both arms, then the head. It throws rocks that you can shoot for silver rings. Hyper lasers make it much easier.

Mission 4

Zoness

Invasion Aftermath

Setting
Toxic waste area
Vehicle
ArwingCorridor
Medal
250 hits
Difficulty
3 of 5

4 of 25

A polluted coast with water below and debris in the air. Searchlights and radar buoys sweep the area, cargo ships carry crates, and Katt Monroe flies in to help.

Boss

Sarumarine. A pirate submarine with heavy armour, so use bombs. Destroy the exhaust pipes first, then bomb the two sides. When one side falls the submarine lowers a crane: destroy that quickly or it rebuilds the side. Shoot its cannonballs to get bombs. Finish the hull with lasers and bombs.

Pickups

  • Gold or silver rings, and sometimes a bomb, from the crates on the cargo ships.
  • Bombs from the submarine’s cannonballs.

Where it leads

Comes from: Aquas. It is the only way in.

Your challenge

  • Destroy every searchlight without being spotted. Magna’s guide counts 30 to 40 of them, and Katt helps with some. If the lights turn red and Fox asks whether you have been spotted, the Sector Z branch is gone.
  • Macbeth, if a searchlight or radar buoy spots you.
  • After the challenge: Sector Z, if none do. You can still pick Macbeth.

What changes with your route

  • Katt repays you at Macbeth and Sector Z, but only if you came through Zoness.

Canyons, ruins and falling pillars, driven in the Landmaster tank. Slippy’s Arwing is caught by the boss and you have to free him. Some players find this harder than Macbeth, because of the swarms and armoured enemies.

Boss

Goras. A huge walking beast. One arm holds Slippy’s Arwing, so bomb that arm to free him, and bomb the other arms and the head too. When its energy bar appears, shoot the red jewel in its chest. Dodge its laser with L or R.

Pickups

  • Nova bombs. Collect them for the boss.
  • Silver rings from skull hovercraft and yellow trees.
  • A gold ring on the third bridge.
  • A bomb in the central area.
  • There are no laser upgrades in Landmaster levels.

Where it leads

Comes from: Sector X, and nowhere else.

  • Bolse. It is the only exit.

The Forever Train

Setting

Vehicle
LandmasterCorridor
Medal
150 hits
Difficulty
3 of 5
Routes
14 of 25

A long supply train carries ore and weapons to Andross’s main base, and you chase it in the Landmaster. There is a ten-minute limit: after that, Mechbeth rams you and the run ends.

Boss

Mechbeth. The easy-path boss. You destroy the whole train by fighting Mechbeth. If you missed any switches, some parts of the fight are randomised.

The derailed train. The hard-path ending. The train goes off the rails and crashes into the fuel bunker, which is worth 50 hits. Mechbeth never appears.

Pickups

  • Silver rings from turrets.
  • A gold ring you hover over.
  • Rings in a building, after the crate from ROB 64.
  • Bombs from elevated guns.

Where it leads

Comes from: Sector X after a quick kill, Solar, or Zoness.

Your challenge

  • Shoot all eight switches along the track, then the switchboard lever near the end. The sixth switch sits behind a gate and is easy to miss. If Falco is alive he usually takes the fourth, and if you came from Zoness, Katt shoots some of them.
  • Bolse, after you destroy the train and fight Mechbeth.
  • After the challenge: Area 6, if you derail the train. You can still pick Bolse.

What changes with your route

  • Katt appears only if you came from Zoness.

A graveyard of wrecked warships, where Andross’s Copperhead missiles are closing on the Great Fox. The Great Fox fights alongside you, and your wingmen shoot at the missiles too unless something is chasing them.

Boss

Copperhead missiles. Six missiles arrive in three waves of one, two and three. Each is worth 10 hits. Your wingmen can steal them, so bomb them at the last second. Ten asteroids are worth 2 hits each. If a missile hits the Great Fox, the level ends.

Pickups

  • Laser upgrades and rings from enemy kills before the missiles arrive.
  • Fly into the Great Fox’s rear bay to restore your wings and shield completely. Lost laser upgrades stay lost.

Where it leads

Comes from: Zoness unseen, or Sector X through the warp.

Your challenge

  • Shoot down all six missiles before one hits the Great Fox.
  • Bolse, if a missile hits the Great Fox.
  • After the challenge: Area 6, if you stop all six.

What changes with your route

  • Katt joins the third wave if you came from Zoness. She does not appear if you came through the Sector X warp, and that keeps her from stealing missile kills. Experts rank this among the hardest medals to earn.

The most heavily defended stretch of space around Venom: minefields, warship fleets and satellites, one after another. The wiki says it leads straight to Andross’s lair and skips many planetside defences. Its 300-hit medal is the highest in the game.

Boss

Gorgon. Shoot its arms to open it, then the pink shield generators, then the centre, and repeat. Dodge the missile salvos. Before its rainbow laser Falco warns you: move to the upper right of the screen, or sit exactly in the centre, where the beam is hollow.

Pickups

  • Bombs are your best tool here, on the minefield and on the linked ships. Save them.
  • A gold ring below one of the pinwheel stations, with a laser upgrade above it.

Where it leads

Comes from: Macbeth or Sector Z, after clearing their challenge.

  • Venom 2. It is the only exit.

Mission 7

Venom 1

Andross’s homeworld

Setting
Temple
Vehicle
ArwingCorridor
Medal
200 hits
Difficulty
4 of 5
Routes
14 of 25

A temple on Andross’s homeworld, reached from Bolse. It opens with a huge swarm, then trenches with rock pillars and falling pillars.

Boss

Golemech. A stone golem. Destroy its legs, arms, torso and head. Once the stone is gone, shoot the red spot on its back.

The robot Andross. Fox goes on alone through a corridor. Shoot Andross’s eyes, then his hands, as in Venom 2, and bomb the mouth when it sucks you in. A mechanical monkey head follows. With hyper lasers you can destroy it before it attacks. Without them, keep shooting until it blows up.

Pickups

  • In the early rock section, a laser upgrade or a gold ring, depending on whether you already have hyper lasers.
  • In the volcano: three gold rings, both laser upgrades and a bomb. Avoid the purple laser bars.
  • An extra Arwing (see Corneria).

Where it leads

Comes from: Bolse.

  • The game ends. The credits show Andross laughing, still alive.

Medal
200 hits

The harder way into Andross’s base, reached from Area 6. There are two fights: Star Wolf again, then Andross himself.

Boss

Star Wolf, again. Upgraded Wolfen II ships that ignore bombs and lock-on lasers. Kill them quickly. StrategyWiki notes a 50-hit combo for a fast kill, and Magna’s guide puts the whole fight at 204 hits.

Andross. Shoot his eyes until he rubs one, shoot the hands while he does, and repeat until both hands are gone. If you take too long he sucks in rocks: bomb or barrel-roll in a corner until he spits them out. Then his brain, in all-range mode. Destroy the eyes first, then hit the green back. It teleports, so U-turn to find it.

Pickups

  • At the volcano entrance, take the tunnels left, right, left, left, right, right for two laser upgrades, a smart bomb and three gold rings. A wrong turn is fatal.
  • StrategyWiki lists an extra Arwing on Venom.

Where it leads

Comes from: Area 6.

  • The game ends. Follow James McCloud out and THE END appears after the credits.

What changes with your route

  • After Andross falls you follow James McCloud out of the lair. He flies slightly faster than you, so boost often. A wrong turn is fatal, but the game saves a checkpoint after Andross falls, so you do not fight him again.

Not covered

Left out: Star Fox 64 3D’s new modes and score-attack tiers, the versus mode, the training course, the full character dialogue and any speedrun glitches. Expert Mode is covered only as far as its unlock. The other Star Fox games are not covered.

What I could not confirm

  • Whether every unlocked branch is a free choice. The sources word the map differently. I draw each as an extra option because that reproduces the wiki’s count of 25 routes. The wiki’s pages spell the choice out for Meteo, Sector Y, Katina, Sector X, Zoness and Macbeth, and I did not find it stated for Corneria, Fichina or Sector Z.
  • How many warp rings Meteo has. The wiki and the 3DS guide say seven. One older GameFAQs walkthrough says six.
  • The Venom medal. Four sources give 200 hits for Venom. The 3DS guide lists 150 for the first version, with a footnote saying players report 300.
  • Searchlight numbers at Zoness. The wiki says each is worth 2 hits while unseen. Magna’s guide counts 30 to 40 lights and gives 3 points each.
  • Where every pickup sits. The guides give rough places only. StrategyWiki lists extra Arwings on Corneria, Sector Y and Venom, but I found no placement for them.
  • Timers. The five minutes at Fichina and the one minute at Katina come from guides, not from the game’s code.
  • Titania’s boss order. One guide says to free Slippy first, another says to start with the other claws, so the page says only that you free him.
  • Wingman events by level, such as who gets shot down where. The sources I could open covered only a few.
  • Difficulty ratings. They are one player’s opinion, from the guide to the 3DS remake. Every route condition I used is also on an N64 source.
  • Pages I could not open: StrategyWiki’s pages for Corneria, Meteo, Fortuna, Katina and Aquas, and the wiki’s Meteo page.
