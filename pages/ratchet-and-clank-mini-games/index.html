The mini-games inside Ratchet & Clank

Fourteen games, from the 2002 original to Rift Apart in 2021, and 64 side games between them. Each entry says what is there, how much of it there is and how much thought it asks for.

A Crack in Time has the most to do. Quest for Booty, All 4 One and Full Frontal Assault have the least. By my ratings, nothing here goes past 3 for depth.

How the ratings work

Amount

  1. 1One or two small things, or nothing I could confirm.
  2. 2A couple of activities you meet only now and then.
  3. 3Several different activities spread through the game.
  4. 4A big share of the game is something other than the main shooting.
  5. 5The widest set in the series, with hours of it.

Depth

  1. 1Press a button, or pick something up.
  2. 2One simple rule: a race, a wave to survive, a quick puzzle.
  3. 3Puzzles that build on themselves, or a system you upgrade.
  4. 4A game with its own strategy. Nothing here reaches this.
  5. 5A full standalone game inside the game. Nothing here reaches this.

These are my own judgment calls, based on the counts and descriptions in the sources at the bottom. They are not an official measure. The main story, the weapons and the shooting are left out on purpose, and so are the multiplayer modes.

2002Nov · No. 1

Ratchet & Clank

PlayStation 2

Amount3 of 5

Depth2 of 5

A handful of breaks from the shooting: hoverboard races, a space level and hacking panels.

  • Hoverboard races

    Racing

    Some races are needed to move on and others are optional.

    More detail

    In Blackwater City you enter the Hovercon Intergalactic Hoverboard Competition. Acceleration is automatic, so the race is about picking a route: blue boost pads and rings give short bursts, and jumping a gap beats following a wide bend.

    Red crates explode and knock you off the board. Once you own the Zoomerator, landing air tricks fills a boost gauge. Winning the championship awards the Platinum Zoomerator.

    The page I read doesn’t say how many races the championship has.

  • Hacking and door minigames

    Most levels

    Hacking and gadget games

    Hacking is one of the minigames you must finish to move on. Others unlock doors, extend bridges or raise platforms.

  • Space and air shooting

    Space flight and combat

    One level is space combat, and another has Ratchet flying through the air to shoot tankers.

  • Clank sections

    Puzzles

    Clank is playable where Ratchet can’t safely explore. He can also control Gadgebots, smaller robots that do jobs for him.

2003Nov · No. 2

Ratchet & Clank: Going Commando

PlayStation 2

Amount4 of 5

Depth3 of 5

The mini-game boom: bike races, ship battles, arenas, Microbot puzzles and Sheep Blasters.

  • Hoverbike races

    Barlow and the Megacorp Games

    Racing

    Collect speed and weapon boosts on the track and use them against rival bikes.

    More detail

    The Megacorp Hoverbike Race on Joba is optional. Winning the first race completes the mission and earns the Charge Boots, and the other hoverbike challenges pay bolts.

  • Ship combat and races

    4 locations

    Space flight and combat

    Ratchet flies the ship to shoot targets or race other pilots. Raritanium buys upgrades at Slim Cognito’s Ship Shack.

    More detail

    Wikipedia notes that some reviewers found the space combat weaker than the rest of the game.

  • Mining ship

    2 planets

    Space flight and combat

    On Tabora and Grelbin, Ratchet mines raritanium from the surface.

  • Gladiator battles

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    Arena fights at the Galactic Gladiators and the Megacorp Games pay bolts. Game Informer praised the arena combat and racing levels.

    More detail

    Wikipedia says the game has four types of “maxi-games”, or minigames: arena battles, spherical world battles, hoverbike races and space races. I found no more detail on the spherical world battles.

  • Electrolyzer and Infiltrator

    Hacking and gadget games

    Gadget minigames. Finishing one switches on a device or opens a door.

    More detail

    A reviewer of the next game said none of its minigames were as bad as these two.

  • Microbot puzzles

    2 times

    Puzzles

    Clank has few moves of his own, but he commands Microbots that can attack, wait, follow him or enter terminals.

  • Giant Clank

    2 times

    Giant Clank and vehicles

    Giant Clank flies to a moon, fires missiles, throws bombs and fights a boss. Unlike the other Clank sections, these can be replayed.

  • Sheep Blasters

    Arcade and oddities

    One minigame, unlocked in challenge mode or by using the Dynamo on the pyramid in Clank’s apartment.

2004Nov · No. 3

Ratchet & Clank: Up Your Arsenal

PlayStation 2

Amount4 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Qwark’s side-scrolling vid-comics and a 99-round arena finale. One review says the races and ship battles are gone.

  • Captain Qwark vid-comics

    5 vid-comics

    Side-scrolling

    Between missions you play a console in your quarters. Each episode is a 2D side-scrolling level starring Qwark in his earlier hero days.

    More detail

    Each episode opens with a cartoon cutscene. One review describes shooting enemies and collecting tokens.

    Five skill points ask you to beat set times in the five vid-comics.

  • Annihilation Nation

    99-round finale

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    A TV show arena where you fight waves of enemies for cash and prizes, some ending in a boss. Gauntlet challenges add a course you must finish while fighting.

    More detail

    The final event, the Qwarktastic Challenge, has 99 rounds plus both bosses. Prizes can be used to upgrade weapons.

  • Deathcourses and Ranger battles

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    A review mentions Deathcourses, and battle missions where you help the Galactic Rangers complete objectives for bolts.

  • Hacker and disguise gadgets

    Hacking and gadget games

    Certain gadgets, such as the Hacker and the Tyhrra-Guise, trigger minigames. One reviewer said none were as bad as Going Commando’s.

  • Gadgebots

    Puzzles

    In Clank’s sections he commands smaller robots through a menu to solve puzzles and attack enemies.

  • Giant Clank and driving

    Giant Clank and vehicles

    Clank grows into a larger form with rockets, bombs and a lot of health. Some planets also have driving sections.

  • Skill points

    30

    Collecting and skill points

    Tasks across the planets, from beating timed runs to turning 15 Floranian blood flies into ducks with the Qwack-O-Ray.

    More detail

    “Feeling Lucky?” is winning the jackpot on a slot machine, and it mostly depends on luck.

    “Go for hang time” is getting at least two seconds of air with the Turbo Slider.

    “Break the Dan” is finding and destroying a hidden Snow Dan in Qwark’s hideout.

    They unlock secrets in the Special menu.

The review that says racing and ship battles are gone also says a racing minigame was reportedly planned but cut. I haven’t found a second source on that.

2005Oct · No. 4

Ratchet: Deadlocked

PlayStation 2

Amount3 of 5

Depth2 of 5

The whole game is a game show, so the extras are Dread Challenges with a gimmick each, plus 165 skill points.

  • Dread Challenges

    Optional

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    Not every course has one, and they often have a unique mechanic. They pay bolts and Dread Points.

    More detail

    Grist for the Mill: survive ten waves of enemies. Two skill points go with it, ten kills in a row with one weapon and five kills without taking damage.

    The Big Sleep: ten rounds with constant spawns, a health drain and high enemy damage. You collect randomly placed nanotech and ammo crates, and on Hero difficulty you have to dodge most attacks.

    Dynamite Baseball: a seven-round arena challenge. Round two sends three Laser Backs at you at once.

  • Maraxus challenges

    6 challenges

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    Four main challenges and two Dread Challenges. The main ones are Jail Break, Total Control, Leave No Man Behind and Showdown in Delta Block.

    More detail

    Total Control is a Puma challenge where you capture three bolt cranks. Showdown in Delta Block ends with a black medal.

  • Speed Trap

    Racing

    One of the Maraxus Dread Challenges: a checkpoint race in the Puma, clearing explosive barrels with your blasters.

  • Spider on a Wire

    Arcade and oddities

    The other Maraxus Dread Challenge: shoot a large number of orbs, including jackpot and explosive ones, with mortars and blasters while Stingers join in.

  • Skill points

    165

    Collecting and skill points

    A maximum of 165, which also reward bolts. Many extras unlock by performing specific feats.

2007Feb · No. 5

Ratchet & Clank: Size Matters

PSP, with a PS2 port in 2008PSP spin-off

Amount3 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Skyboard races, Clank’s vehicle arenas and a rail-shooter space stage, in a story of about six hours.

  • Skyboard races

    Racing

    Racing challenges that pay bolts, gadgets and new armor pieces.

    More detail

    Wikipedia calls them skyboards. Other reviews say hoverboard or skateboard.

  • Clank’s vehicle arenas

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    Arena battles where you play Clank in a variety of vehicles.

  • Giant Clank space shooter

    Space flight and combat

    Space combat is back, but you control Giant Clank instead of a ship. Push Square calls it a rail-based space shooter segment.

  • Gadgebots and Clank Challenges

    Puzzles

    Gadgebots do tasks for Clank in one section of the story. You can use them again in Clank Challenges outside it.

  • Skill points and Titanium Bolts

    25 and 20

    Collecting and skill points

    Skill points unlock cheats, and Titanium Bolts buy Ratchet skins.

The game also has a multiplayer mode, though the PS2 port removes the online part. I haven’t counted it.

2007Oct · No. 6

Ratchet & Clank Future: Tools of Destruction

PlayStation 3

Amount3 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Motion-control extras and a carnival arena. The hacking game is a Sixaxis marble maze.

  • Decryptor hacking

    Hacking and gadget games

    Tilt the Sixaxis controller to roll a metal ball across a circuit board and carry a charge to the end of the course.

    More detail

    Giant Bomb describes it as moving a metal ball between connection spots. The AV Club reviewer said a dozen more optional puzzles like it would have been welcome.

  • Clank and the Zoni

    Puzzles

    Clank can use the Zoni to slow time, slip under a closing door, levitate, rebuild bridges and power devices.

    More detail

    The AV Club says Clank’s solo levels slow the pace but are spread out sparingly.

  • Imperial Fight Festival

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    A gladiator arena inside a carnival park on Mukow, with bosses such as Crushto.

  • Rail-shooter space combat

    Space flight and combat

    In certain levels you pilot a ship against enemy forces. The controls are rail-shooter style, and there are no ship upgrades.

  • Sixaxis flight and gadgets

    Arcade and oddities

    Ratchet free-falls and, with a Clank upgrade, flies, steering by tilting the controller. The Visi-Copter is a remote-controlled helicopter that fires rockets.

  • RYNO IV Holo-Plans

    13 fragments

    Collecting and skill points

    Collecting thirteen Holo-Plan fragments hidden through the game unlocks the R.Y.N.O. IV.

2008Jun · No. 7

Secret Agent Clank

PSP, with a PS2 port in 2009PSP spin-off

Amount4 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Nearly every level is a different genre: a ballroom rhythm game, stealth button prompts and a snowboard run.

  • Ballroom dance

    Rhythm and button prompts

    Countess Ivana Lottabolts challenges Clank to a “dance of death”. Trusted Reviews describes matching button presses to a beat, and calls it great to watch but a struggle.

    More detail

    Wikipedia adds that Clank has to avoid traps during the dance.

  • Stealth takedowns

    Rhythm and button prompts

    Stealth uses quick time events. You press Square, then repeat a button sequence within a time limit. Start from the wrong position and you get a normal attack instead, and a missed input sends you back to the checkpoint.

  • Gadgebot puzzles

    Puzzles

    Special levels where you switch between Gadgebots to press switches, move electric currents and power bridges. Trusted Reviews found them more engaging than much of the main game.

  • Ratchet’s prison fights

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    Ratchet fights his way through rounds in his prison and closes portals with his spanner. Trusted Reviews found it repetitive.

  • Qwark’s tall tales

    Arcade and oddities

    Qwark recreates his battles for his biographer in action levels. Game Revolution says you “unleash your inner Megaman”, and Trusted Reviews found the play a little dull.

  • Snowboard, speedboat and gondolas

    Giant Clank and vehicles

    Clank escapes Glaciara on a snowboard and Venantonio Labs on a speedboat, and jumps across gondolas in Rionosis.

  • Bonus levels

    Collecting and skill points

    You replay them to collect bolts, which upgrade the characters and buy items from vending machines. Trusted Reviews found them hard to feel motivated to play.

2008Aug · No. 8

Ratchet & Clank Future: Quest for Booty

PlayStation Network downloadDownload spin-off

Amount1 of 5

Depth2 of 5

No mini-games that I could find. It is a short tether-puzzle adventure.

  • Tether puzzles

    Puzzles

    The OmniWrench Millennium 12 has a tether that moves obstacles and manipulates objects from a distance. The new platforming and puzzle segments are built around it.

    More detail

    Ratchet can also pick up creatures called heliogrubs and carry them to light up dark areas.

  • Hidden weapon mods

    Collecting and skill points

    Raritanium upgrades are gone. The special modifications they gave are hidden in certain areas instead.

Wikipedia quotes IGN calling it “a quick four-hour experience”. The Fandom wiki says it has no challenge mode.

2009Oct · No. 9

Ratchet & Clank Future: A Crack in Time

PlayStation 3

Amount5 of 5

Depth3 of 5

The widest set in the series: time puzzles, a repair game, space combat, bonus moons and an arena.

  • Time recordings

    Clank’s storyline

    Puzzles

    Clank records copies of himself on colored time pads. A copy can hold a switch down so another copy, or Clank himself, can pass through.

    More detail

    Complex puzzles need several recordings at once. Gaming Nexus says they get harder as the game goes on.

  • Chronoscepter repairs

    Arcade and oddities

    In the Great Clock’s planet rooms you fire a beam at time anomalies while controlling both the planet and the beam.

    More detail

    You destroy “seekers” that try to worsen the damage, and collect power-ups to speed up repairs.

  • Space flight and 2D combat

    Space flight and combat

    Ratchet flies the Aphelion between planets and moons, and in 2D combat sections can destroy asteroids and enemy ships for bolts. Gaming Nexus found the battles an afterthought with awkward controls.

  • Bonus moons

    Platforming

    Small moons are bonus stages. Some are complex 3D platforming levels, compared to Super Mario Galaxy. Others ask you to defeat a set number of aliens or find a hidden weapon piece.

    More detail

    Optional objectives on planets and moons reward bolts, Zoni, Constructo mods, gold bolts or holo-plans for the RYNO V.

  • Agorian Battleplex

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    Cup tournaments and challenges, such as “Bros Before Foes” and “Overkill”, that win you weapons.

    More detail

    Gaming Nexus describes later challenges such as surviving five waves without being hit and fighting two bosses at once.

Gaming Nexus puts the story at about 10 hours, with nearly twice that to find everything.

A Fandom page lists an arcade game called My Blaster Runs Hot. Giant Bomb lists it only as a soundtrack title, so I haven’t counted it.

2011Oct · No. 10

Ratchet & Clank: All 4 One

PlayStation 3Co-op spin-off

Amount1 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Almost no mini-games: six critter-maze rooms that unlock the RYNO VI.

  • Critter puzzle rooms

    6

    Puzzles

    Vacuuming up critters opens hidden teleporters to puzzle rooms. You guide a running critter through a maze by modifying pipes.

    More detail

    If you’re slow, the critter falls and you retry. Completing all the rooms unlocks the RYNO VI.

    The Sixth Axis found them fun but rare.

  • Side-scrolling sections

    Side-scrolling

    These are part of the levels. The Sixth Axis enjoyed them at first, but says they drag on when jetpacks and obstacles are added.

The Sixth Axis review says there is no arena, and calls that a real loss in co-op.

2012Nov · No. 11

Ratchet & Clank: Full Frontal Assault

PlayStation 3, with a Vita release in 2013Spin-off

Amount1 of 5

Depth1 of 5

No side games. The whole game is a tower-defense shooter, and I haven’t counted that as a mini-game.

  • Skill points and gold bolts

    Collecting and skill points

    You can collect both in the campaign. Medals earned from objectives lead to promotions, which grant armor, perks and extra pod deployments.

The main mode has you spend bolts on turrets, mines and barriers to protect your base’s generators. The source I read describes no separate mini-games or challenge modes. The competitive mode is a multiplayer game, which I haven’t counted.

2013Nov · No. 12

Ratchet & Clank: Into the Nexus

PlayStation 3

Amount2 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Clank’s 2D gravity mazes, an arena and a wildlife hunt, in a game reviewers called short.

  • Netherverse mazes

    Side-scrolling

    Clank’s 2D segments are puzzles set in a maze where a Nether chases him. You manipulate gravity to guide him, with the goal of letting the Nether cross the rift.

    More detail

    Game Informer praised the VVVVVV-inspired mini-game. GamesRadar disliked the side-scrolling sections.

  • Destructapalooza

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    An arena tournament on Kragg.

  • Hoverboots wildlife hunt

    Arcade and oddities

    On Thram, the Smuggler rewards you for hunting native wildlife with Hoverboots.

  • Skill points, cheats and gold bolts

    Collecting and skill points

    All three return from earlier games.

GamesRadar estimated about five hours for the whole game.

A separate mobile endless runner, Before the Nexus, came out in December 2013. It lets you earn Raritanium for the main game, and I haven’t counted it.

2016Apr · No. 13

Ratchet & Clank (2016)

PlayStation 4Reimagining

Amount3 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Hoverboard races, Gadgebot puzzles and laser hacking, with no arena.

  • Hoverboard races

    Rilgar and Kalebo III

    Racing

    They work much like the original’s: steer, use speed boosts and do air tricks to extend them. Silver and Gold medals are optional and pay bolts and holocards.

    More detail

    The Rilgar races come in three cups, Bronze, Silver and Gold, and you win each to unlock the next. You can store up to three boosts.

    Silver and Gold add explosive crates that slow you down. A gold bolt is hidden on the track.

    Finishing the Gold Cup in under 1:35 earns the Kalebo Thunder trophy.

  • Gadgebot puzzles

    Puzzles

    Clank picks up Gadgebots and reprograms them into Spring-bots (higher jumps), Bridge-bots (cross gaps) or Power-bots (activate machinery). Other segments have him run from Victor Von Ion, dodging attacks.

  • Trespasser hacking

    Hacking and gadget games

    Align lasers to open the way. A new option deactivates rings that switch off lasers and blockers.

    More detail

    Story hacks can be auto-completed, but you lose the bolt reward and a trophy. Optional Trespasser missions must be completed to unlock hidden items.

  • Holocards

    Collecting and skill points

    Earned from optional objectives and minigame rewards. A set of three unlocks a bonus, such as higher bolt drops or the chance to buy an Omega weapon.

Wikipedia lists spaceship combat missions, but the Fandom wiki treats ship battles as story events, so I haven’t counted them. The Fandom wiki also says there is no arena.

2021Jun · No. 14

Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart

PlayStation 5, with a PC release in 2023

Amount4 of 5

Depth3 of 5

Glitch’s virus hunts, dimensional anomaly puzzles, pocket dimensions and a 15-challenge arena.

  • Glitch hacking

    5 sections

    Hacking and gadget games

    As Glitch you explore a digital world and destroy every Virus Nest, while the minions the nests spawn swarm you. She can walk on any surface, ceilings included.

    More detail

    Three sections are part of the story and two are optional, both tied to Gold Bolts. Gfinity says you can pause and choose Skip Puzzle with no penalty.

    R2 fires cannons, and L2 fires homing Blitz-Infectors, the only weapon that damages nests. X is unlocked later as an area attack.

    Some nests are shielded. You follow a cable to a switch to lower the shield.

  • Dimensional anomaly puzzles

    4 puzzles, 12 courses

    Puzzles

    There are no enemies. You guide a stream of “possibilities” through a labyrinth to a Meta-Terminal, placing spheres in sockets to change the possibilities or the environment.

    More detail

    Clank plays three of the puzzles and Kit plays one. The four spheres are Lift, Speed, Heavy and Electric, and each puzzle needs at least seven possibilities to reach the terminal, according to Gfinity.

    Thumb Culture compares it to Lemmings. The Technovore found the puzzles a refreshing change but said they slow the pace and aren’t optional. Gfinity says you can skip any of them from the pause menu with no penalty, though you miss story dialogue and some crates.

  • Pocket dimensions

    11

    Platforming

    Hidden on the planets. You use the Rift Tether to tear one open, then cross a self-contained platforming challenge to earn an armor piece.

    More detail

    Examples: bouncing across parade floats, wall-running past explosive crates, crossing rotating platforms and riding Speetle courses. Gfinity says combat appears in only one of the eleven.

    The closest thing to a puzzle is the Blizar Prime Mines one, where invisible platforms appear briefly when you hit buttons and you have to memorize the layout.

  • Zurkie’s Battleplex

    3 cups of 5

    Arenas and challenge rounds

    Rivet takes on challenges run by Zurkon Jr. The Bronze, Silver and Gold Cups each hold five challenges, and the last in each is a boss fight.

    More detail

    Tasks include surviving enemy waves with stage hazards, and riding a Speetle through a timed obstacle course.

    Prizes include Bolts, Raritanium, Gold Bolts, Armor Pods, the Box Breaker and a Spybot.

Thumb Culture puts the main game at 10 to 12 hours, with side content stretching it to about 20.

What keeps coming back

Racing
Hoverbikes in Going Commando, skyboards in Size Matters, and hoverboards in the 2002 game and again in 2016.
Arenas
Gladiator battles in Going Commando, Annihilation Nation, Deadlocked’s whole game show, the Imperial Fight Festival, the Agorian Battleplex, Destructapalooza and Zurkie’s Battleplex. The 2016 game has none.
Clank on his own
Gadgebots in the 2002 game, Up Your Arsenal, Size Matters and Secret Agent Clank, Zoni in Tools of Destruction, time recordings in A Crack in Time, and the dimensional anomaly puzzles in Rift Apart.
Hacking
Door minigames in 2002, the Electrolyzer and Infiltrator in Going Commando, the Hacker in Up Your Arsenal, the Decryptor in Tools of Destruction, the Trespasser in 2016, and Glitch in Rift Apart.
Space combat
One level in 2002, four locations in Going Commando, Giant Clank in Size Matters, rail-shooter stages in Tools of Destruction and 2D battles in A Crack in Time.

Not covered

Multiplayer modes, including the online modes in Up Your Arsenal and Deadlocked and the competitive mode in Full Frontal Assault. The mobile games (Going Mobile, Before the Nexus) and the film. Counts come from wikis and reviews that sometimes disagree, and the older games have thinner coverage than the newer ones.
