Playing it on a modern computer (DOSBox)

Optional setup. Skip it if the game already runs for you.

Lost Eden is a DOS game on a CD-ROM, so on a modern computer you play it inside an emulator. The guide’s author used DOSBox. The game reads from the disc, so the disc (or its files) has to be mounted as a CD-ROM drive.

Mounting the disc

Put a work folder on C: first, for example mount C: path-to-your-folder, then mount the disc as D: with the line for your system. See dosbox.com if you have not used DOSBox before.

Windows

mount D: E:\ -t cdrom

Look up your disc drive’s letter first. The guide’s example was E:, so replace that with yours.

macOS

mount D: /Volumes/eden -t cdrom

The folder is the name of the disc as it appears in Finder. The guide wrote /volumes/eden in lowercase.

Linux

mount D: /media/eden -t cdrom

The folder is wherever your system mounts the disc. On many systems that is /media/your-user-name/disc-name.

Installing and running

First time

Z:\>D: D:\>Install

This opens the installation program.

Every time after that

Z:\>D: D:\>Eden

That should do the trick, the guide says.

If it runs too slowly

Press Ctrl + F12 several times to speed it up. The guide says just F12, but DOSBox’s own list of keys puts the speed-up on Ctrl + F12, and I could find no plain F12 action in it. These keys are the ones for DOSBox 0.74.

Useful DOSBox keys
KeysWhat they do
Ctrl + F12Speed up the game (more cycles).
Ctrl + F11Slow it down.
Alt + EnterToggle full screen.
Ctrl + F10Capture or release the mouse.

If you would rather not set it up

GOG sells Lost Eden and says on its store page that the game is powered by DOSBox. That page lists Windows 10 and 11 and says the game will not work on macOS 10.15 and up. I did not buy or run it.

What I checked: I ran the mount lines, the switch to D: and the start of a program in DOSBox 0.74 on Linux, with a folder of test files standing in for the disc. DOSBox accepted all of them. I had no Lost Eden disc, so the installer and the game itself were not tested.

When you are stuck

The Brontosauruses are nowhere in sight
They stand somewhere new in every valley and every game. Keep walking the valley. Pick up mushrooms on the way, since you need one to give them.
Chong says the Tyrann are too close to the citadel
Find a second group of Brontosauruses in Chamaar and have them build one farther away. That is scene 2.3.
A nest will not work on the Triceratops
It must be empty. Give a nest with eggs to Thugg, who eats the eggs and gives back an empty nest.
Eloi has not come to take you to the next valley
He is how you travel between valleys, and he usually turns up soon after you want to leave. He holds back if there is something important left to do in the valley you are in.
The Velociraptors will not take your payment
Each valley wants its own object, and the Eye in the Storm is the wrong one for Koto. Check the table of power objects.
The weapons in Shandovra do nothing
Only one pairing works. Drums to Thugg, bell to Monk, trumpet on Adam. Swap the weapons back through Thugg and Monk if you got it wrong.
You are completely stuck
Use the shell that Tau gave you. It gives hints when you are hopelessly stuck.

What I changed

  • I rewrote every step in my own words. The order, the items and the places are the guide’s.
  • The guide has 19 sections. The page groups them into five parts and 27 scenes. The first Mo section became six scenes, Shandovra became three, and “Two more battles of the Velociraptors” became two, one for Koto and one for Uluru. Every other section is one scene.
  • The page has 132 steps to tick, and each one has a box.
  • I moved the DOS setup into a fold-away section that starts closed. I kept the guide’s three mount lines. I changed the speed key to Ctrl+F12 and wrote the macOS folder as /Volumes. I added the GOG note, the list of useful keys and a line saying what I tested.
  • Spellings: Chamaar (the guide mixes Charmaar and Chamaar), Narrim, Moorkus Rex, Cantura, Tau, Marinda, Despair, Brontosauruses, Pterodactyl. I chose Chamaar because the guide’s own cast list and two other walkthroughs use it. The list after this one shows what each source says.
  • The guide calls the item Monk gives you a “talisman” and the one you hand to Dina an “amulet”. I use talisman for both and add a note where you hand it over.
  • I added one step the guide does not have: taking the flute from Dina. It is tagged Added and has a note.
  • The guide packs several actions into one bullet in places. Each action is its own step here, so each has its own box.
  • The bag of earth from Fugg is a step in Tamara. The guide mentions it only as the Castra’s third answer.
  • New: the route chart, the party chart, the citadel and payment steps, the Castra answers, the Shandovra weapons, the labyrinth routes, the tables of people, power objects and tablets, the “When you are stuck” list, tick boxes, a progress bar, and a way to hide finished scenes.

  • Chamaar: Charmaar in the guide’s headings; Chamaar in its cast list and two other walkthroughs.
  • Narrim: Natrim in the guide’s heading and Narrim in its text; Narrim in another walkthrough.

  • Marinda: Marina twice in the guide.

What I could not confirm

  • I did not play the game. I checked the page against the guide and ran the DOSBox lines in DOSBox 0.74 on Linux, with test files standing in for the disc. I did not run the installer or the game. I only checked the page in Chromium.
  • The guide never says where the flute comes from. Just Adventure and Walkthrough King, as I could read them, both say Dina gives it to you around the mural. I put the step after the prism, but I do not know the exact moment.
  • The moonstone has two sources in the guide: Chong in Chamaar, and Ulan in Uluru. I kept both and say so in the steps.
  • The guide says to give Brontosauruses the larger mushrooms. The hint book (UHS) says to show them to Dina or Mungo and let them tell you which is poisonous. Both are on the page.
  • In scene 1.4 you put the tooth in the mummy’s mouth, and in 1.5 you use it on the skeleton. The guide does not say how you get it back.
  • The guide has five power objects for five valleys. It mentions a mask for Fire in the Clouds and for the Cantura or Tamara object, but not for the Eye in the Storm or the Sky Hammer, so I do not know what the Ulele want for the Sky Hammer. It also does not say where the second of Cantura’s and Tamara’s objects comes from. Walkthrough King, as I read it, lists a sixth object, the River that Winds, and three masks (Bonding, Birth and Death). The last mask the guide says “does not work properly” may belong to that sixth object.
  • The Castra’s answers differ. The guide gives the yellow object from Koto, the moonstone and the bag of earth. Just Adventure, as I read it, names a “Sun Stone” for the first two questions. The Sun Stone may be the yellow object, but I could not tell. I kept the guide’s answers.
  • The guide calls Marinda’s weapons drums, a bell and a trumpet, and calls the thing you take to Jabber a horn. Just Adventure, as I read it, calls the three weapons Rolling Thunder, Chant of Bells and Angry Gods. Walkthrough King says drum, bell and horn. The pairing is the same in all three, but I could not tell whether the guide’s “trumpet” is the same item as their “horn”.
  • The guide’s labyrinth routes come from one playthrough, and the guide hopes they match yours. The other walkthroughs say to follow Shazia and give no turns. Trust Shazia over the picture.
  • The tablets: the guide numbers only the fifth, and calls the last one “the last tablet”. Walkthrough King, as I read it, counts Shazia’s as the fourth. None of the sources I read says where the third comes from, and the total of six is my reading of “fifth” and “last”.
  • The guide’s cast list says Dina joins when you sneak out of the citadel, but the walkthrough has her join in Mo before you leave. I followed the walkthrough. The guide does not say when Mungo leaves, when Monk rejoins, or how often Eloi comes and goes.
  • The guide says Shazia is Adam’s sister. The plot summary on Wikipedia says the Tyrann killed Adam’s mother and sister. I could not reconcile the two, so I kept the guide’s wording.
  • I kept the guide’s spelling “Paraphosaurus” for Dina’s and Mungo’s kind. The guide also spells it “Paraphasaurus”, and I do not know the right form.
  • For the Triceratops the guide says “all valleys”. I read that as the five where you have built citadels, since Shandovra comes later.
  • The DOSBox keys are from the DOSBox wiki’s list for DOSBox 0.74. Other versions may differ. The GOG details are from its store page. I could not open the ScummVM wiki, so I do not say whether ScummVM supports the game.
