The mini-games inside Assassin’s Creed

Sixteen releases, from the 2007 original to the July 2026 Black Flag remake, and 80 side games between them. Each entry says what is there, how much of it there is and how much thought it asks for.

Valhalla has the most to play. Mirage has the least.

How the ratings work

Amount

  1. 1A handful of small tasks.
  2. 2A few repeatable activities, mostly errands or collecting.
  3. 3Several different activities, with dozens of instances between them.
  4. 4Many different activities that take up a large part of the map.
  5. 5More different side games than any other entry, with plenty to do in each.

Depth

  1. 1Press a button, or pick something up.
  2. 2One simple rule or one skill check.
  3. 3Rules or multi-step puzzles you have to learn.
  4. 4A real game with strategy in it, like a dice game or a tower defense.
  5. 5A full standalone game inside the game. Nothing in the series reaches this.

These are my own judgment calls, based on the counts and descriptions in the sources at the bottom. They are not an official measure. The main story, combat and open-world exploring are left out on purpose.

2007Nov

Assassin’s Creed

The Holy Land

Amount2 of 5

Depth1 of 5

Barely any mini-games yet. The extras are errands before each kill, and 420 flags to collect.

  • Flags

    420 flags

    Small missions and collecting

    Scattered across the Holy Land: 20 in Masyaf, then 100 each in the Kingdom, Damascus, Acre and Jerusalem. Some informers pay for a batch of Assassin flags collected against a time limit.

  • Intel errands

    Small missions and collecting

    Eavesdrop on a conversation, pick a pocket or lean on someone for answers. They are short tasks that feed the main hunt, so they feel more like chores than games.

  • Citizens in need

    Fights and arenas

    Fight off city guards who are threatening or harassing townspeople.

  • Templar challenges

    Fights and arenas

    Optional hunts for Templars that have nothing to do with the story.

2009Nov

Assassin’s Creed II

Renaissance Italy

Amount4 of 5

Depth3 of 5

The first real side games: tomb platforming, a run of glyph puzzles and 100 feathers to find.

  • Glyph puzzles

    20 puzzles

    Puzzles and riddles

    Hidden glyphs unlock puzzles that reveal a video called The Truth, one piece at a time.

    More detail

    The puzzles come in a fixed order, so it doesn’t matter which glyph you find first.

    Tasks vary: pick the right paintings, complete a picture, identify an object, find a Piece of Eden, enter a password, or drag something onto a target.

    Unlocking all 20 pieces of the video earns the Vitruvian Man trophy or achievement.

  • Assassin’s tombs

    6 tombs

    Platforming

    Platforming gauntlets with a few guards in them. Each ends at a sarcophagus holding a seal, and six seals open the lock on Altaïr’s armor.

  • Templar lairs

    3 lairs

    Platforming

    Platforming-heavy lairs that end in treasure instead of a seal. They are not in the base game, and how many you get depends on the edition you bought.

  • Feathers

    100 feathers

    Small missions and collecting

    Collectibles spread over the cities. Returning all 100 earns the Auditore cape.

  • Races and courier runs

    Racing and riding

    Races send you through a string of markers before a timer runs out, often over rooftops. Courier jobs are letter deliveries with generous time limits.

  • Beat-ups

    Fights and arenas

    Find a cheating partner and fight until they submit. Later ones give only a general area, so you search with Eagle Vision.

  • Assassination contracts

    2 or 3 per city

    Small missions and collecting

    Contracts from Lorenzo de’ Medici, started at rooftop pigeon coops. Some add conditions, such as no weapons or never being seen.

2010Nov

Assassin’s Creed Brotherhood

Rome

Amount4 of 5

Depth3 of 5

Side content turns into systems: recruit assassins, rebuild Rome and test Leonardo’s war machines.

  • Assassin recruits

    Strategy and management

    Rescue citizens to recruit Assassins, send them on missions around Europe or call them into fights, and spend skill points on them. Recruits can die on missions and don’t come back.

  • Rome restoration

    5 districts, 12 towers

    Strategy and management

    Destroy a Borgia tower to free a district, then renovate it for rewards, much like investing in Monteriggioni in the second game.

  • Romulus lairs

    Platforming

    Underground exploration to find keys to the treasure of a secret cult that worships Romulus.

  • Leonardo’s war machines

    Fights and arenas

    A short run of missions in his inventions, including a cannon-equipped Great Kite and a prototype tank. Afterwards you destroy the machines and their designs.

  • Guild challenges

    3 factions

    Small missions and collecting

    The Courtesans, Thieves and Mercenaries each set their own challenges.

  • Animus training

    Platforming

    Virtual training missions that test combat and free-running skills.

2011Nov

Assassin’s Creed Revelations

Constantinople

Amount3 of 5

Depth4 of 5

Fewer extras, but two of them are whole genres: a tower defense and a first-person puzzle platformer.

  • Den Defense

    Triggered by notoriety

    Strategy and management

    A tower defense. When Templar awareness is maxed, they attack one of your dens. You place crossbowmen and riflemen on rooftops, hold the street with barricades and survive the final wave.

    More detail

    Assassin Leaders on rooftops generate Morale, which pays for more units and unlocks rooftop placement.

    Riflemen fire slower than crossbowmen but pierce armor, so the guide rates them best against the toughest enemies in the final wave. Caltrop bombers slow enemies down near the end of the street.

    Barricades can be upgraded mid-fight to restore their health. Ezio can fire his wrist gun or a cannon, though cannon fire lowers your score and rewards.

    Losing doesn’t lose the den for good: you can retake it by killing the Templar captain. Rewards are rare bomb ingredients, Assassin experience and money.

  • Desmond’s Journey

    5 memory sequences

    Platforming

    First-person puzzle platforming on Animus Island, with Desmond narrating. The Lost Archive DLC adds another campaign in the same style.

  • Bomb crafting

    About 15 bombs

    Strategy and management

    Mix shells and ingredients into smoke, lethal and other bombs. You can only carry three types at once. Piri Reis missions teach what each does.

  • Guild challenges and recruit contracts

    Small missions and collecting

    Challenges for the Thieves, Mercenaries and Romani. Recruits earn levels on contracts, and a top-ranked one can become a Den Master who unlocks a unique mission for each district.

2012Oct

Assassin’s Creed III

Colonial America

Amount3 of 5

Depth3 of 5

The board games arrive: Fanorona, Morris and lawn bowls, with a fight club and frontier hunting around them.

  • Fanorona

    22 pieces a side

    Board and dice

    A capture game on a 9×5 board. Capturing is compulsory and can chain. It unlocks at DNA sequence 5 and is played from the Homestead Manor. Early opponents are tough and later ones much harder.

  • Nine Men’s Morris

    Board and dice

    Each player places nine stones, trying to line up three to form a mill and remove an opponent’s piece. Then you slide pieces. Higher difficulty levels are available.

  • Lawn bowls

    First to 5 points

    Board and dice

    Throw four balls each and get yours closest to the red ball. The easiest of the three, and it unlocks after the fourth Homestead mission.

    More detail

    Most side activities need you to finish a good part of the Homestead missions first.

    Only the bowls field by the Inn counts toward the trophy, not the one in Aquila.

  • Boston Brawlers

    6 Brawlers to beat

    Fights and arenas

    A fight club. You have to beat six Brawlers to enter its tournaments.

  • Hunting

    Hunting and diving

    Hunting animals on the frontier. Black Flag later kept this system.

2012Oct

Assassin’s Creed III: Liberation

New Orleans and the bayouSpin-off

Amount2 of 5

Depth1 of 5

A few Vita touchscreen and tilt gimmicks, and little else.

  • Touchscreen and tilt tasks

    Puzzles and riddles

    Tear open a letter, hold a paper up to the light to find treasure, or tilt a maze like a Labyrinth board. Giant Bomb’s write-up calls them “extremely forced-in.”

2013Oct

Assassin’s Creed IV: Black Flag

The Caribbean

Amount4 of 5

Depth3 of 5

Tavern board games, spear-fishing sharks, a diving bell and a fleet minigame on top of the sailing.

  • Tavern board games

    3 games, 3 skill levels

    Board and dice

    Checkers, Nine Men’s Morris and Fanorona are played at tavern tables. Beginner, intermediate and expert opponents sit in different ports.

  • Shark fishing and harpooning

    Limited spears

    Hunting and diving

    Spear a shark from a rowboat as it tows you along. Miss too often and it breaks free and charges the boat. You can also harpoon large sea animals.

  • Diving bell

    About a minute of air

    Hunting and diving

    Get lowered to wrecks and swim for gold chests. Sharks patrol, and Kenway can’t fight underwater, so you swim away or retreat to the bell. Shipwrecks sit at several marked locations.

  • Hunting and fishing

    Hunting and diving

    The hunting system from the third game stays, and you can fish too. The resources go into crafting equipment upgrades.

  • Edward’s Fleet

    Companion minigame

    Strategy and management

    A fleet minigame that needed the Uplay Passport at launch. That requirement was removed on October 31, 2013.

  • Abstergo hacking

    Puzzles and riddles

    Present-day hacking minigames, similar to the cluster and glyph puzzles from earlier games, reveal secrets about Abstergo.

2014Nov

Assassin’s Creed Rogue

The North Atlantic, River Valley and New York

Amount2 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Ship-and-fort side work. I found no tavern board games here.

  • Assassin hideouts

    10 hideouts

    Small missions and collecting

    Gang hideouts across the three main regions. You kill the leader and burn the flag, and sometimes kill Templar defectors or rescue captured British soldiers.

  • Assassin Interception

    Small missions and collecting

    Catch a messenger pigeon carrying a contract, then find and kill the hidden Assassins before the target dies.

  • Legendary ships

    5 ships

    Fights and arenas

    Hidden boss battles in the North Atlantic.

  • Tavern brawls

    10 taverns

    Fights and arenas

    Beat four thugs in hand-to-hand combat to open a tavern. Afterwards you can recruit crew there.

  • Hunting and fishing

    Hunting and diving

    Gather animal pelts for crafting upgrades.

The sources I checked list taverns only as places to fight and recruit crew. If you know of a board game in Rogue, tell me and I’ll add it.

2014Nov

Assassin’s Creed Unity

Paris

Amount4 of 5

Depth3 of 5

Riddle hunts, murder mysteries, glitch mini-games and a café you can renovate for income.

  • Nostradamus Enigmas

    18 enigmas

    Puzzles and riddles

    Poetic riddles point to symbols hidden around Paris that you reveal with Eagle Vision. Collected fragments open three puzzle rooms beneath the Café Théâtre.

    More detail

    Some enigmas span several districts.

    Fragments feed the cage around Thomas de Carneillon’s Master Assassin armor. Cella Constellatio (4 fragments) asks you to pair pillar symbols with floor symbols. Cella Astri (10) has you rotate planets with three switches. Cella Vaporis (all 18) is a leap across stepping stones between bursts of steam.

  • Murder mysteries

    Puzzles and riddles

    Search a scene with Eagle Vision for clues, talk to the suspect and make an accusation. Accuse the wrong person and you lose some of your reward.

  • Helix Rifts

    Timing and rhythm

    Breaks in the Animus code that play as short mini-games. You collect data while dodging tornadoes or falling rocks, for money and intel.

  • Café Théâtre

    5 tiers, 5 missions

    Strategy and management

    Renovate the café in five tiers. It and your Social Clubs pay into a bank every 20 minutes, and you have to collect the profits. Five café missions raise how much each deposit earns.

    More detail

    Renovating Social Clubs across Paris increases profits and unlocks Social Club missions.

    The Club Hall lets you join or create a club online and earn rewards together.

  • Paris Stories

    Small missions and collecting

    Help citizens with tasks, from tearing down posters to rescuing kidnapped people.

2015Oct

Assassin’s Creed Syndicate

Victorian London

Amount3 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Mostly activity checklists. Fight clubs and street races come closest to mini-games.

  • Fight clubs

    Fights and arenas

    Boxing-style matches in which you face several opponents at once. The bookie Robert Topping runs the gambling around them.

  • Carriage races

    Racing and riding

    Street races between horse-drawn carriages, which are also a way to earn money.

  • Bounty hunts

    17 hunts

    Small missions and collecting

    Enter a restricted area, find the target and kill or kidnap them, then carry them by carriage to a drop-off. Keeping them alive pays more.

  • Child liberations

    10 factories

    Small missions and collecting

    Sneak into a factory, kill the foreman and free the children. No alarms means a bonus.

  • Gang strongholds and Templar hunts

    11 and 17

    Small missions and collecting

    Clear out a stronghold, or track down a named Templar. Killing the Templar in a set way earns a bonus.

  • Gang wars

    6 gang leaders

    Fights and arenas

    Unlocked when you finish every associate activity in a borough. You then fight the gang leader.

  • Train heists

    Small missions and collecting

    Rob trains for money.

Counts come from a walkthrough’s contents list, so treat them as approximate.

2017Oct

Assassin’s Creed Origins

Ptolemaic Egypt

Amount4 of 5

Depth3 of 5

Chariot races, gladiator arenas and two different kinds of riddle hunt.

  • Hippodrome

    8 tournaments

    Racing and riding

    Horse and chariot tournaments that get harder as you go. Each win pays drachmas, and winning Darius’ Great Battle gives you the Roman Warhorse.

  • Gladiator arenas

    2 arenas

    Fights and arenas

    Waves of tougher and tougher enemy mixes, ending in a boss. Krokodilopolis and Cyrene both host fights. Finish every Cyrene event to earn three legendary outfits.

  • Papyrus puzzles

    About 24

    Puzzles and riddles

    A scroll gives a cryptic riddle that points to hidden gear. Work out the place, then search it. The prize is usually legendary gear that scales to your level.

  • Stone circles

    12 circles

    Puzzles and riddles

    Match the star patterns at each circle to finish the Bayek’s Promise quest and open a locked area in the Great Sphinx vault.

  • Tomb and pyramid riddles

    6 Isu mechanisms

    Puzzles and riddles

    Explore tombs and pyramids, solve riddle puzzles for rare loot and activate Isu devices in the vaults to play their messages.

  • Phylakes and war elephants

    10 and 4

    Fights and arenas

    Elite bounty hunters and four armored level-40 elephants. Killing every Phylakes unlocks a quest with a legendary outfit.

  • Trials of the Gods

    Free update

    Fights and arenas

    Free missions in which you fight high-level bosses inspired by Egyptian deities.

Bonus mode: Discovery Tour, added in February 2018, has 75 guided tours of five to 25 minutes each and removes combat entirely.

One guide counts 24 papyrus puzzles, but a citation in Wikipedia mentions 29 scrolls, so treat the number as approximate.

2018Oct

Assassin’s Creed Odyssey

Ancient Greece

Amount4 of 5

Depth3 of 5

A 60-tablet riddle hunt, a wave-fight arena and mass conquest battles.

  • Ainigmata Ostraka

    60 on the main map

    Puzzles and riddles

    Stone tablets carry riddles that describe real places in the game world. Find the place and you upgrade one of 12 engravings.

    More detail

    Each engraving can be upgraded five times, in any order.

    At the top level the bonuses include +50% critical damage at full or low health, +10% critical chance and +20% damage to Overpower abilities.

    Other maps have tablets of their own.

  • The Arena

    Level 22 to 50

    Fights and arenas

    Found on the island of Pephka in the far southeast. It is wave-based fighting with a mini-boss at the end of each wave, and you need level 50 to finish it.

  • Conquest battles

    28 city-states

    Fights and arenas

    Mass battles for Sparta or Athens. You weaken a region by killing its leader, burning supplies or looting nation chests, then join the attackers or the defenders.

  • Mercenaries

    50+ mercenaries

    Fights and arenas

    Crimes put a bounty on you, and mercenaries come to collect. You can fight them, pay up, or assassinate whoever posted the bounty.

  • Legendary animals

    Hunting and diving

    Hunt rare beasts on land.

Bonus mode: Discovery Tour: Ancient Greece (September 2019) is an educational mode with free roaming or historian-led tours. The Fate of Atlantis expansion adds three regions, each with a series of trials.

2020Nov

Assassin’s Creed Valhalla

England and Norway

Amount5 of 5

Depth4 of 5

The series high. It has a full dice game, rap battles, drinking contests and fishing.

  • Orlog

    19 opponents

    Board and dice

    A dice game of attack, defense and gods’ favors, played against 19 people in the base game: 2 in Norway and 17 in England. You win by wiping out the other player’s health.

    More detail

    Each player starts with 15 health. The dice show axes, arrows, helmets, shields and hands.

    A turn allows up to three rolls, then a God Favor phase and a resolution phase.

    Dice with gold print earn God Tokens, and a hand steals one from your opponent. Tokens pay for favors such as Thor’s Strike (damage), Idun’s Rejuvenation (healing) and Vidar’s Might (strips helmets).

    A physical Orlog dice game was also sold.

  • Flyting

    About 16 locations

    Timing and rhythm

    Viking rap battles. You pick the reply that rhymes and matches the rhythm of the insult, against a timer.

    More detail

    Winning raises your Charisma, which opens more dialogue options, and some choices can change the story.

    You can bet on a match. One launch-era guide listed 16 flyting locations and warned that the list might be incomplete.

  • Drinking contests

    First to 4 cups

    Timing and rhythm

    Tap along in rhythm as the tempo climbs. A mistake resets the speed. You can bet 100, 150 or 200 silver and the winner takes it all.

  • Fishing

    19 fish types

    Hunting and diving

    Build a Fishing Hut in Ravensthorpe, then cast, hook and reel against line tension. Too much strain snaps the line. 15 types live in England and 4 in Norway.

  • Alpha animals

    Hunting and diving

    Legendary beasts to hunt down. A trophy asks you to defeat all of them.

The trophy list also names standing-stone puzzles, cairn challenges and 10 Animus anomalies. I didn’t look into how those play.

2023Oct

Assassin’s Creed Mirage

Baghdad

Amount1 of 5

Depth2 of 5

The lightest entry. It has riddle treasure hunts and contracts, but no mini-games that I could find.

  • Enigmas

    12 enigmas

    Puzzles and riddles

    A map, riddle or letter points to buried treasure that isn’t marked on your map. You search for the glowing spot and dig. Rewards are talismans or outfit dyes.

  • Contracts

    19 contracts

    Small missions and collecting

    Each has a main mission and an optional sub-task, such as killing no one, staying unseen or taking no damage.

  • Tales of Baghdad

    6 tales

    Small missions and collecting

    Small stories you tend to stumble across rather than seek out.

  • Pickpocketing

    Small missions and collecting

    One review liked it at first but found skill points make it much easier. It yields materials and tokens.

A review I read describes much of the rest as “box-ticking exercises.” It also doesn’t mention any mini-games.

2025Mar

Assassin’s Creed Shadows

Feudal Japan

Amount4 of 5

Depth2 of 5

Around 195 shrine, temple, castle and training activities. Most are quick, and one is a meditation rhythm game.

  • Kuji-kiri

    22 (Naoe)

    Timing and rhythm

    Naoe meditates while you hit four buttons in time, then repeat the growing sequence from memory.

    More detail

    A white circle must land in a grey zone as you press each button. The sequence grows, the symbols fade and you have to remember them.

    Some steps need two buttons at once, and the rhythm speeds up near the end. A mistake partly resets the sequence.

    You can switch to a simpler arrow-key input, or to Auto, which completes it for you.

    Finding four unlocks a Naoe quest, and finishing all 22 unlocks the Zen Master achievement.

  • Katas

    18 (Yasuke)

    Timing and rhythm

    Timed quick-time sequences in which Yasuke performs a chain of combat moves.

  • Horse archery

    10 (Yasuke)

    Racing and riding

    Shoot targets from horseback along a set path. Rewards include experience and a new horse design.

  • Sumi-e painting

    24

    Calm and creative

    Paint landscapes and scenes as a calm break from combat. You can hang a painting in your hideout study.

  • Kofun tombs

    10 tombs

    Platforming

    Tombs full of traps and puzzles, with level-scaled legendary gear at the end.

  • Hidden trails

    10 (Naoe)

    Platforming

    Grappling-hook platforming routes. Chests along the way often hold rare gear.

  • Temples, shrines and castles

    36, 27 and 38

    Small missions and collecting

    Collect lost pages at temples, pray at shrines and infiltrate fortified castles. Temples and shrines give knowledge points and experience, and castles give a large amount of experience.

Domains, added in the final major update on June 16, 2026, is an endgame mode: a replayable Animus simulation with five challenge maps.

The 195 total adds up one guide’s counts across nine activity types.

2026Jul

Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced

The CaribbeanRemake

Amount3 of 5

Depth3 of 5

The three tavern games come back with silver wagers, and diving gets bigger.

  • Tavern board games

    11 taverns

    Board and dice

    Checkers, Nine Men’s Morris and Fanorona return. You pick one of three wagers before each match, and the stakes rise with the opponent’s skill.

    More detail

    Ordering a drink starts a brawl with five enemies, and beating them unlocks the tavern. Havana and Nassau unlock through the story, and Great Inagua costs 100 reales to restore.

    Checkers wagers in reales, by opponent skill:

    WagerBeginnerIntermediateExpert
    “I’ll wager my luck.”100200300
    “Aye, let’s see what you got.”200400600
    “You are outmatched, lad!”300800900

    The Grandmaster trophy needs wins at each tavern game, and one guide says beating each beginner opponent once is enough.

  • Diving

    Hunting and diving

    You can dive almost anywhere to explore, and some treasure moved to dive spots. A reviewer says this ties into the Smuggler’s Coves.

I could confirm the board games and the bigger diving. The sources I found don’t say whether the original’s harpooning, shark fishing or fleet minigame came back, so I haven’t counted them.

What keeps coming back

Board and dice
Fanorona and Nine Men’s Morris first show up in III. Black Flag adds checkers, and Resynced keeps all three. Valhalla’s Orlog is the one dice game, and the most involved game of the lot.
Riddle treasure hunts
Nostradamus Enigmas in Unity, papyrus puzzles in Origins, Ainigmata Ostraka in Odyssey and Enigmas in Mirage all ask you to read a riddle, work out the place and go there.
Fight clubs and arenas
The Boston Brawlers in III, Syndicate’s fight clubs, Origins’ Hippodrome and arenas, and Odyssey’s Arena.
Rhythm and memory
Valhalla’s drinking contests and flyting, and Shadows’ Kuji-kiri.
Small management games
Recruiting assassins and rebuilding Rome in Brotherhood, Den Defense in Revelations, and the Café Théâtre in Unity.

Not covered

Other spin-offs (the handheld, mobile, browser, side-scrolling and VR titles), most downloadable expansions, and the multiplayer modes. Counts come from different guides, so they can disagree a little.
